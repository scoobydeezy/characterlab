## Finite authored source
Actors are performer, blocker-a, blocker-b; observers are A and B. One action is
deliver. There are at most eight attempts, identified by integers1..8 in an authored
catalogue. These numbers are source protocol identities, never causal evidence.
The original contains attempt, competence Boolean, optional blocker, and each
observer's outcome/obstruction access. It contains no learned belief or conclusion.

Every attempt starts from an already frozen deliver intent. The source produces an
attempt with the same intent. A blocker yields failed execution; otherwise competent
execution succeeds and incompetent execution fails. Block and incompetence may coexist:
observing obstruction does not establish that removing it would have ensured success.
Truth retains competence and actual blocker. Neither is copied into failure-only views.
This component receives the fixed chosen intent; it does not manufacture or qualify
the upstream decision/dice/expression. Public integration must consume the real frozen
output and prove its invariance under changed execution inputs.

## Observation and explanation
Denied access gives no corresponding observation, including no denied-event marker.
Outcome access supplies success/failure. Obstruction access supplies the visible
blocking act and observer-local actor token only if a blocking act actually occurs.
No blocking act seen is absence of evidence, not evidence of no obstruction.
The narrow apparatus independently identifies each visible actor and marks attempts
with a visible local episode cue. The same cue identifies later reports to that
observer. Local cue and actor tokens differ between A/B. There is no general SEM
recognition or implicit cross-observer linkage claim.

Reports have an independently visible receipt1..8, attempt, positive/negative claim
about obstruction and recipient set. Reports are controlled utterances; their claims
are not derived from truth and can be false. This source does not model a chosen
communicative goal. Empty recipient sets produce no observations. Reusing a receipt
requires the same attempt/claim; changing recipients is permitted as later exposure
of the same statement. A different receipt is independent only in this apparatus.

## Evidence and bounded alternatives
Evidence accepts an observer's own obstruction observation or report, never an
outcome alone. It preserves local episode, source kind, visible source key, polarity
and (for witnessed obstruction only) local actor token. An observation from another
observer rejects. A report's negative claim does not erase an earlier observation.
Truth and research IDs are not inputs to this consumer.

GroupedMean and LastClaim maintain unique source support separately per local
episode. First evidence establishes known belief; missing evidence leaves unknown.
Direct witnessed obstruction contributes1; a report contributes1 or0. Duplicate
source keys do not update; contradictory reuse rejects. GroupedMean is the exact
integer positive count divided by support count. LastClaim uses the last newly
admitted source polarity. Neither diagnostic is calibrated confidence. One witnessed
positive followed by a negative report gives1/2 versus0; neither is selected as a
general trust law. Do not give direct evidence an unearned credibility weight.
OutcomeOnly is the explicit ablation returning no evidence from any observation.
SuccessAsIntent and OmniscientCause remain required public comparison controls;
they are not permissions in this kernel.
