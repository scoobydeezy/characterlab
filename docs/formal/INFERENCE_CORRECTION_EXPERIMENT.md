## Exact proposition and authority
The proposition is whether obstruction occurred during a particular observer-local
delivery episode, represented by existing978 in that observer's979 knowledge. It is
not personal blame, the cause of every failed action, counterfactual success or
the identity of an inferred culprit. A failed attempt due to incompetence with no
blocker can be followed by a false positive obstruction claim. Later negative claims
must update that same episode proposition without rewriting intent, expression,
outcome or the earlier observations. Outcome-only evidence must not infer the cause.

received testimony. No reference mechanism is retired or imported.
