# General attention canonical recall projection

`general-attention-recall-projection/0.1-candidate` implements the accepted
event/body partition and retention-metadata erasure over the permanently allocated
carriers. Local representational implementation; no new psychological edge.

An admitted prior SurvivingEpisodeLedger is structurally decoded in full before
partitioning. Every acquisition must belong to the supplied admitted observer, have
a unique acquisition identity in that leaf and precede the receiving instant.
The event projection copies calibration and each surviving child's positive evidence;
the body projection copies each surviving signal group's positive evidence.
RecalledAcquisitionEvidence preserves the exact acquisition identity, observer,
formation instant, source selection and transformation version. No identity is
allocated. No missing child is reconstructed.

UseProtection and OutcomeSignificanceDirections are absent from the returned grammar.
They cannot influence ranking through this projection. Returned records are detached
canonical values. Empty partitions return an empty list without changing the owner.

The owner leaf and observer are trusted admitted inputs at this component boundary.
This function does not authenticate the observer, prove source provenance, validate
all historical formation relations, resolve PRJ/IDN or authorize a ReadDomain.
The future public accessor must enforce those prerequisites and available-cue gating
before reading the leaf. In particular, calling this function is not public admission.
Goal-baseline and ordinary body recall may share the projection while retaining
separate policies and occurrence production. General Attention remains OPEN.
