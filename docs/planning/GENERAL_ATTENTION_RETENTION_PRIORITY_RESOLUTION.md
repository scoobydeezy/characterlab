# Recency Retention Baseline: A accepted

2026-09-12. **FIRST SURVIVAL-PRIORITY BASELINE ACCEPTED by supplied planning ruling.**
Resolves the [decision packet](GENERAL_ATTENTION_RETENTION_PRIORITY_DECISION.md).
It does not accept a theory of importance, whole public shape or numeric allocation.

Within each accepted non-borrowing kind partition, preferentially preserve the most
recently acquired semantic units. The sole age operand is original authoritative
acquisition time. Retrieval, presentation, accessibility change, consolidation
processing and prior survival never refresh that time. Partial fragmentation preserves
both acquisition identity and original time for every surviving child.

Equal-time units use a stable canonical acquisition/child address order solely for
deterministic reconciliation. The exact direction and canonical encoding must be
specified in the component/public contracts; opaque identity is not chronological
or psychological importance. Psychological survival comparisons should avoid ties;
mechanics tests may deliberately exercise deterministic tie resolution.

Plan once over prior survivors plus the complete declared same-barrier acquisition
candidate set. Do not append/evict sequentially in handler order. The ordinary-memory
authority reconciles one result; no partition borrowing, competing old-leaf replacement
or hidden access to provisional sibling state follows.

Zero cognitive capacity removes all current units in that partition through the
modeled policy. It is not an invalid workload. Source, byte and settlement-work safety
bounds remain independently validated. The exact formation/retention composition
must distinguish newly proposed content from committed historical acquisition and
current survivors; the pure policy cannot itself assert a successful formation.

Use the name **Recency Retention Baseline**. It means newest-first survival under
scarcity, not importance, reward, benefit or causal significance. No encoding-strength,
pressure, attribution or current-accessibility field is repurposed. It preserves the
accepted whole-semantic-child loss rule, including all views of a surviving body group.

## Mandatory richer comparison remains open

Retrieval/use → acquired retention protection is a mandatory distinct comparison,
not an optional enhancement. Before broad ordinary-memory qualification or a reduction
verdict treats recency as sufficient, instantiate and compare a qualified protection/
reinforcement candidate. General Attention cannot close on this baseline alone.

The candidate must separately decide whether credit requires recall, actual use,
consequential/successful use, or outcome-linked consolidation; whether credit is
unit-local; and how repeated self-triggered recall avoids automatic immortality.
Presentation count does not silently answer those questions.

Preserve three discriminating cases: newer unused content versus old repeatedly used
content; a self-rehearsal feedback loop; and old rarely retrieved but strongly
outcome-consolidated content. Recency, use reinforcement and outcome-derived significance
must remain distinguishable. Supported attribution alone does not establish the last.
No particular reinforcement equation or outcome-importance law is accepted here.

## Required baseline checks, not yet passed

- Earlier acquisition loses against later acquisition under same-kind pressure.
- Equal-time ties are deterministic under input/member permutation.
- Partial survivors retain exact child bytes, acquisition identity and original time.
- Same-barrier admission order does not affect the survivor set.
- Zero capacity causes loss; independent kind capacity remains unaffected.
- Presentation/accessibility/consolidation metadata cannot refresh original age.
- Policy output composes with the existing loss component; no removed payload archive
  or replacement/fragment identity is created.

Existing eight fragmentation tests and seven source mutations qualify loss mechanics
only. They do not yet qualify recency selection, public admission, acquired protection
or persistence. Next: exact bounded component law and tests, then owner/carrier
composition and public proof. Historical accepted profiles remain unchanged.
