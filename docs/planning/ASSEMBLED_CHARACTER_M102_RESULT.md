## Proposed experiment, not an adopted law

Recommendation: compare current truncation with **signed stochastic rounding of
the standing modifier**, for every standing source under one successor policy.
Retain D3, SourceCoverage, relevance, zero-base suppression, unit1/4, cap3,
base dice, context/situation modifiers, thresholds and the Auto rule.

Let h=clamp(standing.net/unit,-3,3), a=abs(h), n=floor(a), Q=2^32 and
p=floor(Q*(a-n))/Q. Use modifier sign(h)*n with probability1-p and
sign(h)*(n+1) with probabilityp. At p=0 no rounding draw is needed.
Convolve that mixture with the existing die, then apply the existing direction
and other modifiers. Positive standing strengthens an Avoid reason in its own
direction; it must not be treated as an unconditional action bonus.

This is an engineering candidate, no verdict. It preserves zero/integer/capped
cases and sign symmetry without multiplying learned gains. Mean modifier error
relative to h is less than1/Q and toward zero; a tiny residual dead zone remains.
Exact unquantized fractional mixing would require arbitrary rational sampling:
the current oracle caps span at2^32, and actual history denominators can exceed it.
The proposed Q comes from that existing boundary, not outcome tuning. A uniform
Q-span draw has no rejection/fallback because Q divides the oracle's2^128 range.
Do not quietly substitute this finite-grid law for exact unquantized rounding.

One illustrative exact arithmetic command gives, for equal d4 reasons:
P(A wins)=1/2 at h0;1031/2048 at h=1/64;1017/2048 at h=-1/64;
23/32 at h=1. Normalization, sign/direction, integer and cap controls pass.
The captured M89 source magnitude would yield a nonzero rounding mass, whereas
its old modifier is0. These are arithmetic illustrations, not native behavior,
psychological qualification or evidence of a changed executed choice.

Cost: the live sampler currently rolls a uniform face and adds an integer.
Changing only analytical distributions would be a defect. A successor needs a
separate authenticated rounding-purpose draw for each fractional active reason,
ledger capacity, truthful versioned nucleus/draw records and matching analytical
and sampled scoring. Existing face addresses stay separate; paired comparisons
must couple corresponding rounding events explicitly. Model/save identity and
affected RNG/trace/admission/continuation contracts need review. No substrate RNG
algorithm, separate runtime or persistent psychological owner is proposed.
