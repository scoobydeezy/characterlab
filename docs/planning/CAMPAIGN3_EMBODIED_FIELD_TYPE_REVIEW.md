names, insertion order or a numeric ID's magnitude.

## 4. Closed union layouts

**EmbodiedSample** is a schema-discriminated type alias over exactly the two newly
allocated sample record schemas. It allocates no wrapper record or extra tag. Canonical
record identity already discriminates present versus unavailable. Every unknown schema
and every wrong schema version rejects. This follows the existing closed-record-union
technique rather than implying universal CanonicalValue admission.

**PressureResult** is one new record: required VariantTag, conditionally present Value.
Known requires Value:rational; Unavailable forbids Value. Unknown tags and extra fields
reject; a sentinel zero or presence Boolean is not an absent Value.

**LevelChainCarrier** is one new record: required VariantTag and Sample:EmbodiedSample,
conditionally present ReservedExperienceId. Present requires a present Sample and a
real reserved1106 identity; Unavailable requires an unavailable Sample and forbids the
reservation field. A mismatched tag/sample pair rejects. ReservedExperienceId authenticates
nothing alone. Numeric tags for these new unions await the separate allocation gate.

These two union records plus LevelPressureIngressDefinition increase the record inventory
from28 to31; EmbodiedSample remains an alias, not a32nd record. Earlier three symbolic
unions therefore resolve to two wire records and one closed schema alias.

## 5. Identity-role refinements

Observer1000, channel1005, modality1006, definition1027, seam1036, event1001, unit1039,
mutation authority1025, observation1115 and experience1106 retain their accepted families.
CharacterId uses1002 with accepted character qualification. PressureOccurrenceId remains
one new unallocated family. Definition references additionally resolve the exact expected
kind/version: body registry, reserve parameters, channel, pressure, sampling source or
replenishment definition as named by the field. No generic definition lookup enters
the pressure function.

Role validation applies at every nested field and map/set position as required by VAL.
Structural typed-identifier admission is not permission to use another namespace.
No identity is inferred from a field name, ordinal position, spelling or another ID.
The new fixed fuel member and modality/event/version payloads remain SYM-2 decisions.
