## Proposed A: adopted bodily maintenance goal

A character holds a finite prospective commitment to maintain an observed bodily
signal within a desired interval. Unlike the old acquisition task, an in-range
observation does not retire it. It remains active until a separately governed end
or withdrawal. This difference is a new lifecycle hypothesis requiring acceptance.

Provisional semantic inventory (field names are not record/namespace allocations):

| Surface | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Concern key | Existing qualified CharacterId plus a goal identity; exact reusable identity family still to be inventoried |
| Criterion | Qualified observer-safe signal domain and desired closed interval within that domain; no physical unit conversion or REG mapping |
| Lifecycle | Adopted active window and status, produced/seeded by an explicit goal-admission contract; no intensity or authored importance |
| Read projection | Exact same-subject goal status/criterion at consequence time, through a bounded registered read capability |
| Assessment basis | Existing consequence occurrence plus immutable concern version/value projection and actual read provenance; no live resolver |
| Assessment result | Unavailable or an assessed qualitative relation; a future occurrence must identify the committed result without duplicating existing identities |

Initial state may represent a genuinely adopted goal only if the receiving profile
explicitly admits that premise. It is not evidence of a learned preference, an act of
autonomous adoption or developmental emergence. A test changing this state changes
the admitted goal, not physical truth or a free-floating importance coefficient.
Later adoption/withdrawal machinery needs its own governed source and transaction.

## Proposed assessment to review after ownership choice

The smallest candidate is a concern-satisfaction boundary assessment, not a magnitude
score. For a closed admitted evidence interval E=[l,u] and desired interval G=[g,h]:

    Within    iff g <= l and u <= h
    Outside   iff u < g or l > h
    Ambiguous otherwise

Missing concern, inactive/unavailable evaluation context or missing evidence yields
Unavailable. Ambiguous does too for this first conservative candidate. Do not take
a midpoint or treat missing concern as assessed indifference. Closed bounds can
conservatively enclose accepted half-open samples; source projection must prove it.

Using the same consequence-time concern for admitted before/after evidence, propose:

| Before | After | Proposed result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outside | Within | Attainment |
| Within | Outside | Violation |
| Within | Within | NoSatisfactionBoundaryChange |
| Outside | Outside | NoSatisfactionBoundaryChange |
| Either unavailable/ambiguous | Any | Unavailable |

These labels distinguish evidence availability and direction without a scalar.
NoSatisfactionBoundaryChange is an assessed lack of this candidate's selected event,
not a universal judgment that the outcome was unimportant. Continued severe failure,
near misses, gradual improvement and many defining events would be missed. The
positive-increase contrastive attribution candidate also does not generally provide
a target for bodily decreases; generic Violation examples are not public witnesses.

The proposal intentionally exposes its reduction: only crossing satisfaction status
would be a significance candidate. It is NOT accepted by the source-direction ruling.
If this misses the intended first bodily concern, choose a different assessment law
before implementing; do not silently redefine a goal to fit the fixture.

## Historical integrity and independent branches

At consequence assessment time, bind the actually admitted concern and evidence to
the immutable assessment. Later withdrawal or revision changes future assessments
only. This may be an immutable projection plus accepted provenance, not necessarily
a new occurrence for every concern read. It must not become a forgotten-payload
archive or an unrestricted state resolver.

Keep the significance assessment independent of attribution. Only downstream target
credit joins its result with the actual Supported attribution and an extant retained
target. Unavailable attribution prevents that credit, without falsifying an otherwise
available concern assessment. Preserve false attribution and original encoding bytes.
