# Ordinary acquisition identity: B accepted

2026-09-12. **SEMANTIC ROLE ACCEPTED by supplied planning ruling.** This resolves
the alternatives in the [role audit](ATTENTION_ACQUISITION_IDENTITY_ROLE_REVIEW.md).
It is not whole-shape acceptance, permanent allocation or runtime qualification.

Choose B: AcquisitionOccurrenceId identifies successful creation of one durable
ordinary-memory acquisition. SelectionOccurrenceId retains its accepted selection
meaning, including empty results, and remains explicit source provenance. The two
roles are not aliases even where a profile admits a one-to-one relationship.

A nonempty admitted formation may reserve its candidate occurrence within the
transaction. It becomes authoritative only on successful whole-instant settlement.
Empty selection, unavailable/zero formation, rejected formation and rolled-back
settlement commit no acquisition occurrence or retained acquisition. Rollback must
restore allocator and pending work as well as the memory append. A candidate record
in diagnostic failure inspection is not authoritative successful-acquisition history.

New event/continuant and interoceptive acquisitions share this semantic occurrence
family, with distinct typed child payloads. Exactly one new acquisition role is
accepted; no additional MemoryEpisodeId, FormationId, VisualEpisodeId,
InteroceptiveEpisodeId or per-group SignalGroupId follows. Body groups are addressed
by `(AcquisitionOccurrenceId, InteroceptiveSignalId)`. The precise learning-evidence
and output carrier that owns the occurrence remains a whole-shape obligation;
allocating a redundant formation identity is not an acceptable shortcut.

Retained acquisition preserves its admitted selection provenance and qualified
CharacterId. Recollection, consolidation, retention and pruning address the acquisition
occurrence. Lifecycle B keeps initial retained bytes historical and records subsequent
changes separately. Source, selection and acquisition identities grant no authority
to resolve discarded source samples. Read access remains governed by subject, admitted
producer, exact retained content and ordinary-memory permissions.

The first bounded profile permits **at most one initial acquisition per qualified
admitted selection source**. This is a profile cardinality rule, not a global identity
equivalence or a requirement to form memory from every selection. A later reviewed
profile may allow splitting without redefining either identity. No split is admitted
by the current ruling.

Existing accepted source-addressed measurement memory remains unchanged. The old
bounded attention diagnostic, its1143 role, permanent tables, models and runtime also
remain unchanged. Alternative A is preserved in the audit as a coherent unchosen
design, not retired as invalid.

## Forward implementation obligations

The visual draft's retained key, presentation references and recall targets must move
to the acquisition role, retaining selection only as provenance. Body memory must use
the same occurrence role without being forced into visual fields. Occurrence/output
accounting must add one conditional candidate per successful nonempty formation and
prove zero committed occurrences on failure. Same-instant formations still reconcile
under one family writer; the new identity does not repair competing leaf overwrites.

The [identity propagation delta](ATTENTION_ACQUISITION_IDENTITY_PROPAGATION_REV1.json)
is a bounded machine-readable edit inventory. It does not claim that the older visual
packet already contains the new body/lifecycle closure. Old packet bytes and receipts
are preserved; their numeric bounds cannot be treated as current integrated bounds.

Public proof remains required for no-content exclusion, producer authenticity,
replay uniqueness, subject isolation, same-instant reconciliation, rollback, later
target stability, pruning without identity reuse and end-to-end safe-source invariance.
The five SEM opportunity tests concern experience identity only and do not qualify
these acquisition obligations. General Attention remains OPEN.
