# Separate bounded interoceptive durable encoding: accepted direction

2026-09-12. Records the user's supplied planning analysis. **Topology subdecision
ACCEPTED.** General ATTN-001 remains OPEN. Lifecycle B and attribution-gated credit
are unchanged. The historical decision request remains unchanged as history.

Use a separate bounded interoceptive durable-acquisition gate first. Visual/event
and body evidence remain distinct unit domains with separately declared permissions,
eligibility and capacity. Do not invent an event/continuant or role for a body sample.
Do not introduce a visual/body priority exchange rate through a codec or shared list.

Permitted body observation and transient experienced pressure do not imply durable
retention. The body gate must exhibit actual within-modality contention: more eligible
candidates than capacity causes at least one operand to miss acquisition. A singleton
allow-list cannot qualify this obligation. Holding body-gate inputs fixed while adding
visual competitors produces no direct displacement in this first model.

Selected body evidence must enter first-class durable memory under explicit ownership,
retention and retrieval rules. A peer retained representation or generalized episode
carrier remains a layout question; neither permits a secret remembered-values dictionary.
Unselected, denied or unavailable sample bytes cannot later be recovered from an
observation archive by attribution, consolidation or another learning consumer.

This does not establish universally independent attention resources. Preserve shared
acquisition and shared-control allocation as explicit future competing models. Later
workspace/control may introduce mixed-modality competition. No such mechanism or
reduction is accepted here, and visual distraction is not ruled out as a phenomenon.

Freeze the within-body contention and cross-modal invariance pair, plus the
transient-pressure-without-durable-retention control. Also retain exact sample-byte
carriage, source authenticity, selected-only later access, unavailable versus zero,
proper observer/subject binding and rollback/restore obligations. None is publicly
passed by this architectural ruling. Proceed with component, source and whole-seam
work under standing authorization; no reapproval of this topology is needed.
