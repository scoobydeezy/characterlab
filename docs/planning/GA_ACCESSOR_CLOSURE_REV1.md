All six new character/observer owner leaves remain present when empty. Missing leaves
are invalid admitted state, not a cue absence or a zero-capacity result. The two
derived recall views preserve actual acquisition identity, source-selection identity,
formation time, observer and surviving positive evidence. They omit retention use
protection and directional-significance metadata. No graph, presentation history,
physical source or protocol history is smuggled into either evidence view.

`recall-partition-projection/0.1-candidate` now implements the corresponding bounded
component erasure and partition rule. The canonical adapter still must preserve the
additional canonical source metadata from the admitted retained carrier. It must not
reconstruct that metadata from opaque ordinals or a source archive. The component's
string child keys remain validation labels, not new canonical perceptual identities.

Available-cue gating precedes the episode/graph/presentation reads. Body rank reads
neither graph nor event presentation state. An absent cue reads none of those roots;
PRJ/IDN admission is a separate operation and is not waived. Goal-baseline recall
uses the same body evidence projection but its own admitted policy, evaluation and
published recollection occurrences. Sharing a projection does not share a budget.

The ordinary owner reads the full B0 ledger because it owns protection, fragmentation
and retention. The graph owner reads only its own graph. Presentation formation and
cleanup may consume the ordinary owner's validated survivor descriptor through the
accepted batch coordinator; they do not read a sibling's tentative state. Attribution,
cueing, publication, use dispatch and significance join get actual completed operands
and no episode read accessor of their own.

Inherited SEM counters/active-file accessors retain their exact existing 241/242
paths and observer key rules. They are not aliases of the new last-window accessors.
Inherited task-status and measurement-prediction accessors retain their accepted
paths and absent-prior behavior. Local-reserve sampling retains the physical-source
adapter and exact channel-to-reserve binding; a cognitive ranker receives no physical
accessor. Formation protocol admission/reconciliation remains a runtime hook outside
character ReadDomain and uses no new cognitive projection member.

Public registration must bind each of these projections to an exact admitted root,
field, selector, return grammar and transition ReadDomain. A family name or a checked
namespace alone is insufficient. That compiler and its wrong-subject, absent-cue,
wrong-root, tampered-derived-result and rollback controls remain OPEN.
