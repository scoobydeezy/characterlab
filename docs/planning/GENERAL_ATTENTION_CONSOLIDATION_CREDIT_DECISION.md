# General attention: what gives an earlier episode consolidation credit?

2026-09-12. **OPEN architectural decision.** Lifecycle B remains accepted.
No accepted initial-encoding bytes, EVID contract or qualified model is reopened.

## Decision in one example

The character encodes a lamp, then experiences bodily relief. The lamp was visible,
but no interaction connecting it to the relief was perceived and no admitted prior
causal knowledge connects them.

May temporal co-occurrence alone produce the first **outcome-linked consolidation**
of that lamp episode, or must an explicit fallible event-to-outcome attribution first
provide the credit assignment? These predict different memory histories for the
same permitted observations. This is a choice of cognitive mechanism and prerequisite
topology, not an identity, scheduler or calibration decision.

## Recommended first contract: attribution-gated consolidation

Require a separately traceable, character-side attribution assessment before an
event receives outcome-dependent credit. It may be wrong about objective causation;
it must expose its permitted evidence and rule. Temporal proximity or common subject
alone is insufficient in the first profile. A lack of support yields unavailable
credit, not zero realized effect and not an invented causal target.

Proposed causal path, pending this ruling and subsequent exact seam review:

    admitted consequence evidence + permitted prior/interaction evidence
      → fallible outcome attribution and evidence-valued effect assessment
      → consolidation assessment targeting an already encoded episode
      → append-only consolidation under its registered owner

The conservative EVID269/270 path remains intact beneath this assessment. Initial
encoding proceeds without waiting. No character transition reads replenishment
truth, source dispatch ancestry or an omniscient effect ledger. The new assessment
does not backdate a Cause role into the original SEM envelope. A valid target must
use the existing retained selection address and accepted subject projection.

This choice best preserves MEC-007's requirement that outcome-dependent relevance
apply to causally connected material, while replacing the historical truth-side
EffectProvenance shortcut with a separate fallible character process. It expands
the work to include a minimal causal-attribution source/assessment and appropriate
positive, false-attribution and insufficient-evidence controls before consolidation
can earn a positive qualification. It does not require a general causal graph or
final Need ontology merely to begin that investigation.

## Alternative: experiential association without causal credit

The first contract could instead allow noticed pressure change to modulate an
explicitly bounded set of recently encoded episodes through temporal association.
The output would mean “encoded around this experienced change,” never “caused it.”
The lamp can then gain consolidation history without a supported causal relation.

This is a legitimate distinct hypothesis, but it needs an explicit temporal
eligibility law and must keep pressure change distinct from realized regulatory
effect. It cannot discharge the historical causal-relevance control by renaming
association as attribution. That control and the later attribution seam remain open.
Choosing this alternative does not authorize closing General Attention early.

Both mechanisms could eventually exist, with distinct records and interventions.
The question is which is authorized as the first outcome-linked consolidation path;
one must not silently stand in for the other in the initial whole-seam claim.

## Why the existing ruling does not select this

Accepted B separates acquisition from later consolidation and forbids rewriting or
resurrection. It expressly leaves the later mechanism to be qualified. It does not
say whether temporal association is sufficient credit or causal interpretation is a
prerequisite. The Architecture distinguishes association, expectation and causal
learning; those separate boxes should not be collapsed through an implementation
default. SEM-001G currently excludes arbitrary event-to-event causality.

The [source inspection and executed counterexamples](ATTENTION_CONSOLIDATION_SOURCE_REVIEW_REV1.md)
also show why exact hidden Applied cannot simply fill the gap. Equal safe body bins
and pressures can conceal Applied10 versus1. A pressure difference, a net level
change and an event-attributed effect have different meanings. No choice grants a
truth oracle or promotes a speculative effect law.

## Prepared proof obligations and limits

Under the recommended contract, freeze these additional obligations for the later
whole seam: unsupported temporal neighbour gets no causal credit; supported false
attribution remains possible under hidden-truth intervention; equal safe operands
yield equal assessments; foreign subject/observer and unencoded targets reject;
unavailable support differs from known zero; original episode bytes remain exact;
and an attribution-removal intervention isolates the downstream consolidation effect.
These are NOT PASSED and do not specify a new numeric law.

The scheduler side no longer needs a decision: six new actual-scheduler fixtures
support immediate base formation and separate atomic consolidation. Thirteen combined
tests pass. Production source contracts, field inventories, allocations, source
authenticity, restore, model packaging and corpus qualification remain pending.

**Requested ruling:** proceed first with attribution-gated consolidation (recommended),
or first with explicitly noncausal experiential association while retaining the
causal-credit obligation for a subsequent seam. No reapproval of lifecycle B is needed.
