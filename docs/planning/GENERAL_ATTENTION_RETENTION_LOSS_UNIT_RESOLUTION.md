## Governed scarcity unit

One admitted retained semantic child unit consumes one unit of its kind's cognitive
retention capacity. Event/continuant acquisition uses the existing semantic encoded
unit key. Interoceptive acquisition uses one retained InteroceptiveSignalId group,
regardless of its bounded number of supporting views. The full address includes the
AcquisitionOccurrenceId: the same signal in two acquisitions is not one shared slot.

Records, byte size, evidence-view count and presentation labels are not cognitive
cost units. Splitting a record, changing serialization, duplicating a view or inventing
signal aliases cannot change cognitive capacity usage. Identity/member admission must
prevent unitization tricks; it does not merge legitimately distinct experienced signals
merely because their values match. Separate finite byte/work/source bounds still apply.

The accepted non-borrowing per-kind partitions remain unchanged. This cost rule does
not choose SurvivalPriority: age, encoding strength, importance, attribution, use and
accessibility are not implied eviction operands. In particular current accessibility
remains excluded from the first retention policy.

## Complete-child loss and stable identity

The first loss operation removes a complete retained semantic child. A nonempty
surviving subset keeps the original AcquisitionOccurrenceId, exact surviving child
bytes and existing child addresses. No fragment ID, replacement acquisition or new
presentation is created by loss. The operation cannot merge, rewrite or resynthesize
surviving units.

Within-child evidence degradation is deferred. A surviving body signal group keeps
all its admitted retained views, including contradictory or differently bounded views.
Removing one inconvenient view is not authorized fragmentation. Event-unit internal
evidence likewise remains intact under this first loss operation.

Final-child loss removes the acquisition as a current individually recallable ordinary-
memory target. Its historical occurrence remains historical, and its identity cannot
be reused. Historical occurrence, current retained content and current accessibility
are three distinct conditions. Governance/replay metadata must be bounded and cannot
retain a hidden copy of forgotten evidence.

Removed content is absent from current memory and unavailable to ordinary retrieval,
attribution and presentation. Neither a producer ID nor historical trace authorizes
its recovery. Diagnostic history may identify a removed child; it is not a sample
resolver. Historical initial output bytes remain governed evidence of the original
commit, not character-readable surviving memory.

## Preserve the comparator

Whole-acquisition retirement remains a named simpler future comparison: its cost is
an acquisition and its loss removes that whole acquisition. The accepted semantic-unit
model instead counts surviving children and can preserve a partial acquisition.
The alternative is not retired as incorrect. Within-unit degradation and semantic
consolidation/reconstruction are separate future mechanisms, not passed by unit loss.

MEC-010 remains CONTROL+CORPUS for accessibility and reinforcement. Its historical
immutable-content implementation supplies no loss law. No reference source changes
or imports are authorized, and no preservation obligation is dropped.

## Required public witnesses — frozen, not passed

1. At least one acquisition begins with two or more retained units; valid same-kind
   pressure removes some but not all. Later recall returns a survivor, cannot return
   a removed unit, and uses the same AcquisitionOccurrenceId.
2. Both kinds independently encounter real same-kind pressure. Partition capacity
   cannot be qualified by retain-all fixtures or workload rejection.
3. Extra bounded views of one signal do not consume extra cognitive slots; surviving
   group evidence bytes stay exact. Serialization changes cannot change cost.
4. Final-child loss removes the individual recall target without identity reuse or
   source-history reconstruction of the removed content.
5. Unchanged other-kind retention operands remain unaffected by pressure in this
   kind. Current accessibility variation alone does not cause loss.
6. Formation and loss reconcile under the one ordinary-memory authority, with exact
   auxiliary-state disposition, rollback, replay and bounded persistent metadata.

These are new proof obligations. Prior acquisition/scheduler tests are append-only
fixtures and do not pass partial survival, forgetting, fragmentation or public reads.
