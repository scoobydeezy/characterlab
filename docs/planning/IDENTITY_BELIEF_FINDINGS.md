## Represented belief is not the acquired standing fold

The unchanged qualified task source retains actual choices, frozen contextual
expressions, qualifications and its weighted standing. The new self estimate has
its own admitted evidence and unknown state. Withhold self evidence while retaining
exactly the same source biography: self belief becomes unknown, while standing
remains present. StandingAlias cannot express that distinction. It is retained as
an explicit reduction countermodel, not a candidate selected for production.

The represented proposition is a bounded positive task-fidelity tendency. Each
accepted nonzero source expression supplies a signed polarity. Mean estimates that
polarity; it does not reuse the standing fold or its authorship-weighted magnitude.
Context and authorship still gate the source's eligibility. The binary projection,
equal observation weights and likelihood mapping are candidate choices, not a
general trait extractor or calibrated prediction of future behavior.

## Observer evidence and self evidence are independent

Each observer receives only its own controlled semantic observations. Opposite
reports can produce an estimate contrary to self-concept with the same actual
biography. Removing self evidence leaves the lawful observer's entire view unchanged;
PrivateOracle instead leaks the self-state change. Changing hidden source biography
while withholding observer reports likewise preserves the lawful whole view and
defeats the oracle. These are component view comparisons, not native public proofs.

In the correction case, target actions remain A/A/A/A. Observer A first receives
two false negative reports, then two correct positive reports: its Mean trajectory
is-1,-1,-1/3,0. The earlier false observations and judgments remain in the log.
Self and observer B remain+1. This is later contradictory evidence changing the
current estimate, not retroactive repair of a past observation or calibrated trust.
Reports use a fixed identity-established source; recognition, vocabulary, learned
reliability and general social interpretation remain unqualified.

## Consumer and timing limits

Post-update prospective appraisal consumes only the represented estimate and current
goal. A goal-only intervention preserves all evidence, source actions and beliefs,
but changes adverse likelihood. SplitExposure is reused with its other factors
fixed. No new social action is sampled; the new belief is not added as a second
standing bonus to the source decision. New component sequencing does not qualify
native scheduler phases or state ownership. Those are the next admission gate.

Failed execution preserves all safe views and beliefs. Forced movement and trivial
source behavior supply no identity evidence. NoLearning keeps observations in trace
without acquiring a belief. These controls retain absence, failure, disagreement,
known neutral and unknown as separate states.
