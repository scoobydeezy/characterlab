## A — separate child-local directional presence set (recommended)

Propose a finite set per currently surviving child containing zero, one or both of:
MovingCloser and MovingFarther. A committed qualified, attributed credit inserts
its direction. Duplicate/repeated credit has no accumulating numerical effect.
The set is independent of UseProtection and original encoding/acquisition time.
It records that each kind of concern-relative consequence has earned credit, not
its magnitude, frequency, current concern intensity or affective valence.

Keep exact assessment provenance under the eventual governed result/history contract;
the two direction tags are not a substitute for authenticating result/target binding.
No payload archive or duplicate occurrence identity is implied. Loss removes the
child's local significance state; later same-semantic acquisition starts empty.
Withdrawal does not erase historical earned credit or change prior assessments.

This leaves later survival policy able to distinguish closer-only, farther-only and
both without replaying an archive. It does not yet grant any direction higher priority.
Absence of a tag means no retained earned credit of that direction, not assessed
zero significance and not proof that no assessment ever occurred.

## B — one shared child-local significance bit

Propose one Boolean set by either qualifying direction, keeping direction only in
the committed assessment provenance. This is smaller but deliberately makes current
retained significance state insensitive to which direction earned credit. A later
direction-specific retention policy would require a state successor or an explicitly
authorized historical read; it cannot silently recover that distinction from traces.

## Discriminator and proof obligations

Compare two matched retained children with equal use bit, age, capacity and one
qualified attribution each. One receives MovingCloser, the other MovingFarther.
A retains different local significance values; B stores the same Boolean. These
need not produce different survival until a separately accepted policy consumes them.

Under either proposal: same result replay cannot amplify credit; late transaction
failure rolls back significance and result together; no sibling/control credit;
lost-child credit rejects; new acquisition receives no inherited state; old concern
withdrawal leaves historical assessment and prior credit intact. These are proposed
vectors, not passed controls. Numeric source/result allocation remains gated.

## Separate source gap and following policy decision

Neither choice authenticates the causal-target projection. The four-trial attribution
source still needs its same-observer candidate identity, episode/child binding and
actual committed result. Supplying a target address to the normalizer proves none
of those. No significance application is authorized until that join is contracted.

This choice also does not decide whether significance outranks use, combines with
it, or shares a tier, nor any memory-budget exemption. Recommend A to retain both
accepted directions in bounded state without a score, preserving B as a reduction
candidate. The subsequent composed retention rule remains an explicit decision.
