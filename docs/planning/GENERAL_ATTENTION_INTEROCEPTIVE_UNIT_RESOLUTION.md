# Signal-per-opportunity acquisition: accepted direction

2026-09-12. **Acquisition-unit subdecision ACCEPTED**, from the user's supplied
planning review. General ATTN-001 remains OPEN. Separate bounded body acquisition,
lifecycle B and attribution-gated consolidation remain accepted and unchanged.

One slot represents one observer-side bodily signal in one admitted sensing
opportunity. Multiple channels of that declared signal contribute a bounded evidence
basis, not extra competing units. Distinct experienced signals remain distinct even
when they share a hidden physiological cause. The safe observation model owns the
grouping; equal values, units, similar names and hidden source paths do not establish it.

Opportunity identity comes from actual admitted sensing, not timestamp equality.
Different opportunities remain distinct at the same instant. No new namespace or
parallel occurrence allocator is requested by the review's illustrative OpportunityId.
The public inventory must inspect and reuse an appropriate existing sensing identity
through an explicit admission/projection rule.

Grouping is not evidence integration. Preserve each admitted sample's identity,
censoring, resolution, provenance and exact value. No averaging, intersection,
most-precise selection, voting or uncertainty reduction is licensed. Later consumers
may read only the bounded basis that was selected and retained, not the original
sensing stream. Different views of one opportunity are not independent repeated trials.

Candidate count, selected count, views admitted per signal and retained payload volume
need separate finite bounds. This ruling chooses no public numeric values. A component
may exercise explicit test limits; those are not permanent model commitments.

Retain sample-counting acquisition as an explicit competing control. Its implemented
contract, source and receipts remain unchanged. Shared acquisition/control remains a
separate future resource hypothesis, not retired or silently merged into this gate.

Freeze the paired obligations: an added admitted view of one signal leaves candidate
count/slot use unchanged while its retained basis may differ; three actual signal
units at capacity2 genuinely contend. Also freeze same-time distinct-opportunity
separation, exact contradictory-basis preservation, bounded payloads, authentic safe
grouping and selected-only later access. No public source or memory qualification
follows from this architectural acceptance.
