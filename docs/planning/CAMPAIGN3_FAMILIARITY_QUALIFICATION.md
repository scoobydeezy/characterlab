does not change a full signature match. No previous same-category imprint gives
NoHistory; prior imprints with no comparable features give Unknown; an unobserved

individual with disjoint displayed features can feel novel. Denied versus absent
first encounters also preserve full later public provenance, including occurrence IDs.
These are epistemic boundaries, not claims of accurate instance recognition.
