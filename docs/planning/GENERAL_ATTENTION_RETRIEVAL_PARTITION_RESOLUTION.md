# Separate ordinary recall partitions: A accepted

2026-09-12. **ACCEPTED by supplied planning ruling.** Resolves
[retrieval partition decision](GENERAL_ATTENTION_RETRIEVAL_PARTITION_DECISION.md).

Event/continuant and interoceptive recall have independent bounded candidate/slot
partitions under one ordinary-memory service and shared identity/read/persistence
governance. They neither borrow slots nor compare scores across kinds. Results of
one kind do not become scoring operands for the other without a new explicit mechanism.

Event recall retains direct and learned-spreading paths. Body recall is direct-only
initially and requires a separately reviewed signal-cue/accessibility law. No body
graph or event-score copy is authorized. Sample count, interval width, pressure,
depletion, physical identity and channel count do not become body recall strength.

Both partitions must exhibit within-kind contention. With this kind's cue, retained
state and operands fixed, adding other-kind competitors cannot directly alter its
selected result. This does not deny later distraction or shared workspace/control.
The union of typed successful reads is not simultaneous conscious/workspace admission.
Any common downstream consumer must declare its own bounded semantics.

Shared cross-kind recall B remains a serious comparator requiring an explicitly
calibrated common allocation law. The six partition component cases establish only
prefix/resource enforcement on supplied ranked identities. Body law and public recall
qualification remain open, as do broader General Attention and acquired protection.
