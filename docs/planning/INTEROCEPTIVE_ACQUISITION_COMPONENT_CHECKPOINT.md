## Implemented bounded control

`interoceptive-selection-component/0.1-candidate` validates and detaches actual
EMB461/463 values, requires one observer/instant and unique observation/channel IDs,
then applies capacity0..2 to at most three candidates. Equal priority and canonical
channel tie order are explicit control semantics. Absent samples are ineligible;
present samples are not filtered out merely because current pressure would be zero.

Only selected canonical bytes enter the opaque one-use view. The trace-side audit
contains identities/dispositions, not a payload resolver. Input mutation, copied or
forged views, reuse and closing are tested. No private full observation history is
retained by the view. No permanent memory representation or source authority is
claimed by this component.

[Seven tests pass](INTEROCEPTIVE_SELECTION_TESTS_REV2.json); the preceding revision1
failed in fixture construction because rational denominators were omitted. The fixture
was corrected, not the canonical rational contract. [Six actual source substitutions](INTEROCEPTIVE_SELECTION_REVIEW_REV1.json)
are detected: retain-all, ignore-capacity, select-absent, reusable view, ignored
observer/time and duplicate channels. This qualifies IB-A..G at component scope only.

The cross-modal test varies the actual visual selector's candidates while keeping
body component inputs fixed. The within-body test adds candidates that displace the
previously sole selected sample. These exercise distinct component paths; they do
not qualify an integrated public multi-channel producer or durable acquisition.

## Source granularity finding

Current EMB has one reserve anchor and one selected body signal/channel in its frozen
public profile. Its461 payload has observation, observer, channel, instant and interval,
but no safe physiological-signal grouping key. Distinct channel IDs alone cannot prove
different underlying bodily quantities. The component intentionally makes no such claim.

Two further tests use actual reserveBin, EMB codec and the new selector. With hidden
reserve27 and capacity100, channels of widths1/5/10 yield27..28,25..30 and20..30.
The sample-level control treats these as three candidates; at capacity2 the third
loses acquisition. Giving all three channels the same width still consumes two slots
and displaces the remaining duplicate readout. This is the defined control behavior,
not a selector implementation defect or proof of distinct bodily sensations.

[Combined report](INTEROCEPTIVE_ACQUISITION_COMPONENT_TESTS_REV1.json):20 tests pass
across body selection7, granularity2, retained contrastive attribution9 and retained
support-only attention boundary2. Previous24-test receipts retain their own scopes.
No test count is promoted to a full-suite or public-memory claim.

The [candidate-unit decision](GENERAL_ATTENTION_INTEROCEPTIVE_UNIT_DECISION.md) therefore
needs a disposition before public source packaging. Adding arbitrary aliases of the
one existing sensor solely to satisfy “capacity+1” would conceal this resource-model
choice. Selecting a signal-group unit instead requires explicit safe grouping and an
inhabited multi-signal source. Existing raw physiology, pressure and visual contracts
remain unchanged under either choice.

## Durable representation work prepared

The recommended layout direction after unit closure is a peer retained acquisition
in the ordinary episodic family, with an immutable admitted sample basis and declared
formation authority. It must use existing observation identities for its operands,
qualified CharacterId for ownership, and a governed later memory reader. No generic
archive resolver or fabricated perceptual body continuant is needed. Its precise key,
selected-result provenance, absence branches, query semantics and history bounds are
not yet whole-shape accepted; no sidecar store has been implemented.

Only actual retained operands may populate a contrastive trial. Trace availability,
transient pressure or knowledge that a sample occurred does not authorize recovering
discarded bytes. Correlated readings of one sampling opportunity cannot count as
independent repeated trials. Baseline/response pairing must come from permitted
evidence under a separate source contract, never hidden dispatch ancestry.

No public source, persistent write, new schema/allocation, model, restore or corpus
promotion occurred. Source/version/hash receipts and old public qualification remain
preserved. The shared-acquisition/shared-control alternatives are retained candidates.
