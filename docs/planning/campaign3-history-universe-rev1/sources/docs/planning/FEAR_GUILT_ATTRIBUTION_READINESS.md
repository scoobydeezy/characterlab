# Fear and guilt attribution readiness — 2026-09-24

LOCAL DISPOSITION. Stage A intake only; no new allocation or qualification.
Next bounded social frontier: Brief12.10 clause3, misreading fear as guilt.
Private affect, observable emotional display and observer explanation of that display
must remain separate. Fear is not direct evidence of hidden wrongdoing or its cause.

## Bounded question
Can an observer infer guilt from an admitted fear-related cue and be wrong because
the target is afraid for a different reason? Can additional observer-visible context
revise that attribution while the observed cue and target's actual guilt stay fixed?
The experiment should explain the mistaken inference, not encode the correct answer
in a label or repair it with hidden truth.

Use a controlled target, named event and finite competing explanations. The source
must produce or admit the observable cue independently of any guilt verdict.
EMOTIONAL_DISPLAY supplies a relevant private-affect/observable-cue precedent;
PERSONSTATE supplies separate current inference and retained impression. Neither
alone establishes evidence about the cause of a cue or trust in an explanation.
Keep actual wrongdoing, private fear, produced display, perceived evidence and
observer attribution separately traceable. No moral character inference from one cue.

## Required comparisons and gate
Hold cue/context fixed while hidden wrongdoing or private cause changes; whole later
observer views must match. Hold private truth fixed while admitted context changes.
Include innocent fear, unavailable cue, unavailable context, contradictory evidence,
two observers, later revision and unchanged historical judgments. Distinguish a
simple cue-based attribution candidate from a context-sensitive alternative and a
truth Oracle. Do not call a mistaken but evidence-relative inference an information
leak; do not call a truth-reading correction lawful inference.

update and read domains, timing and bounded appraisal/action consumer first.
