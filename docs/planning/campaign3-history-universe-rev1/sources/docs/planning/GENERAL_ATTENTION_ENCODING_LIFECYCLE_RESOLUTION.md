# General attention encoding lifecycle: accepted B

2026-09-12. **Architectural direction accepted: B.** Records the planning review
supplied by the user, selecting immediate durable encoding followed by separate
outcome-linked consolidation. General ATTN-001 remains OPEN.

This resolves the choice in [the historical decision request](GENERAL_ATTENTION_OUTCOME_ENCODING_DECISION.md).
That proposal, including its recommendation of A, remains unchanged as history.
This receipt supersedes its open disposition and recommendation, not its substrate
findings. No numeric allocation, complete seam acceptance or runtime qualification
is implied.

## Accepted boundary

Initial encoding records acquisition-time cognition. Only evidence and admissible
prior information available then may determine its historical factors. Durable
formation does not wait for an expected future consequence. Here “immediate” means
the acquisition transaction's normal durable commit, not a new persistent write at
phase40 or visibility to other handlers before quiescence.

Later observer-safe experience may produce a separate append-only consolidation
operation over an encoded episode. It cannot rewrite selected evidence, initial
factors, encoding assessment or what the observer knew at acquisition. It is not a
second episode and must not count as another presentation of the original unless a
separately accepted law explicitly defines such a presentation.

Outcome-dependent historical Need relevance is a **candidate consolidation law**.
It is not automatically accepted mathematics and cannot be supplied by TaskConcern.
Perceptual surprise and consequence surprise are separate time-local assessments;
each needs its own admissible evidence and prior. Neither hidden outcome truth nor
later causal interpretation can become an earlier encoding factor.

Consolidation cannot create an original episode that was not encoded. A later
observable outcome can itself be encoded under its own acquisition contract.
Reconstructing an earlier event from that outcome remains a separate future seam.

Keep immutable episode content, append-only consolidation provenance, current
derived/materialized memory state, and contextual accessibility distinguishable.
The ruling chooses this lifecycle, not a scalar shared by importance, retention,
recognition and accessibility. No particular strengthening, association,
fragmentation or reorganization formula has been accepted here.

## Same-instant implementation constraint

Logical order remains acquisition assessment, safe consequence assessment, base
episode formation, then outcome-linked consolidation, even within one instant.
The actual scheduler's phase140 batch members are terminal. Therefore this ruling
does not authorize a formation handler to enqueue another phase140 handler.

The successor must explicitly reconcile a new base and its consolidation within one
atomic barrier while preserving separate derivations and mutation authorities.
Uncommitted sibling writes are not ordinary owner-readable state. A proposed base
may only be used through a declared, validated batch composition rule; existing
common-B0 declarations alone do not supply that rule. Failure must publish neither
the base nor consolidation, and restore must reproduce their separate provenance.

For a consequence in a later instant, the original must already be durable and
recallable under the accepted retrieval rules. Lack of a later consequence must not
cancel, delay or erase that original encoding.

Reuse the draft's SelectionOccurrenceId episode address. The review's illustrative
episode/consolidation numbers are not a request for a duplicate EpisodeId, occurrence
namespace or allocator. Consolidation occurrence identity, exact source references
and storage declarations require separate symbolic inventory before allocation.

## Adversarial review and next work

Architecture §8 and North Star §11 support the separation of memory content,
retention, accessibility and later consolidation. Architecture's learning boundary
still requires CharacterLearningEvidence and registered family mutation authority;
this ruling does not add appraisal fields to the accepted minimal OutcomeEvaluation.
The existing consequence SemanticExperience → OutcomeEvaluation →
CharacterLearningEvidence route must remain intact.

The current32 draft recipes disable Need and surprise. They remain valid bounded
comparison proposals but cannot qualify the new consolidation path or close General
Attention. Existing139-event/172-output/152-slot bounds exclude this new path and
must not be reused as its total-work proof.

Next design work is an observer-safe consequence/target association and assessment
contract, followed by exact lifecycle carriers, owner reads, batch composition and
competing consolidation laws. Same observer, qualified CharacterId and chronological
order are necessary but insufficient evidence that a consequence concerns a
particular episode. A truth event ID, proximity, matching glyph or shared subject
must not silently provide that association. Ambiguous or absent evidence must stay
unavailable; it cannot become zero effect or an invented target.

MEC-007/CTL-004 preserve the historical salience mechanism as a control, with its
outcome-dependent part investigated downstream. MEC-008/009/010 still require
distinct association, access, decay and reinforcement comparisons. RET-003/006/010
continue to prohibit clipped censored surprise, truth Overflow salience and
commitment-as-Need. This lifecycle decision retires none of those obligations.

## Frozen proof obligations — not passed

| Vector | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| EL-B1 | Recall before a later consequence can use the already committed original. |
| EL-B2 | No consequence ever arrives: original encoding still commits normally. |
| EL-B3 | Equal acquisition evidence and different later outcomes preserve identical initial episode bytes and factors. |
| EL-B4 | An excluded or zero-retained original cannot be resurrected by consolidation. |
| EL-B5 | A consolidation adds no duplicate original episode or implicit presentation. |
| EL-B6 | Missing, ambiguous, foreign-observer or foreign-subject target evidence cannot consolidate an episode. |
| EL-B7 | Same-instant formation/consolidation preserves separate causal records and one atomic commit; no terminal child emission. |
| EL-B8 | Failure in either same-instant operation rolls back all roots, queue and allocators. |
| EL-B9 | Whole-prefix restore reproduces initial bytes, consolidation history and subsequent access. |
| EL-B10 | Perceptual and consequence surprise use their respective evidence times; later evidence cannot revise the earlier assessment. |
| EL-B11 | Hidden truth changes with identical admitted evidence cannot alter character-side consolidation. |
| EL-B12 | No-consolidation control preserves initial encoding and isolates the qualified later effect. |

Exact lawful effects, observer-safe effect measurement/connection sources, category
and Cause/Incidental sources, public wrappers, finite model recipes, numeric gates
