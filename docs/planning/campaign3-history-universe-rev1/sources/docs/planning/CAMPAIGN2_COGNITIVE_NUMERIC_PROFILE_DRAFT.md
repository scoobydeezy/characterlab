# Cognitive numeric profile proposal

Status: DRAFT, not a model/parameter freeze or numeric-ID allocation. Exact arithmetic
continues to use accepted bigint/rational substrate operations; no floating-point
approximation, generic precision truncation or new RNG mapper is introduced.

## Proposed admitted bounds

The exact twenty-one model recipes supply their literal calibration values; the
public factory does not admit arbitrary in-range replacements. S0 keeps the accepted
ADAPT/IDN domains,0..2 adopted task statuses,0..2 immutable plan bindings, and empty
prediction/memory/identity learning state as required by their receiving profiles.

Original input is at most100 entries. Deliberation times are signed integers1..100;
probe times are1..99 so their accepted T+1 read/recall remains at most100. At most one
of these physical/cognitive originals occurs at an instant. Dependencies retain the
declared empty-list grammar. Task deadlines are the exact recipe values, at most100.
These restrictions imply the input-count ceiling rather than silently permitting
an unbounded list of coincident opportunities.

Workspace capacity0..3; at most2 selected tasks/origins/candidates; at most6 raw signals;
at most2 active nuclei. Prediction and identity support retain their64-entry caps.
The exact source schemas govern all collection members. Syntactically admitted input
can still encounter a legitimate dynamic state/reference/capacity failure; the bounds
do not promise that every possible sequence succeeds.

The proposed scheduler ceiling is27 executed events per instant, derived from the
work-accounting draft and the eligibility invariant. Its actual deadline-overlap
control must pass, and a too-small ceiling must fail atomically. A chosen protocol
allocates24 runtime ordinals; with two carried read slots the instant maximum is26.
Across at most100 originals, a safe whole-prefix ceiling is2400 additional runtime
ordinals and2402 total scheduled events including the two initial private deadlines.
Probe future children are already included in its generation count. These are bounds
above the accepted initial allocator values, not a new initial ordinal convention.

At most3 draws per decision, each at most3 hash candidates; at most300 draws/900
candidate computations across100 originals. These are not scheduler events. Each
face span is one of4/6/8/10/12 and tie span2. Exact analytical PMFs have at most two
independent dice and at most144 elementary combinations. CDF/convolution results are
exact, and fair tie sharing stays separate from the accepted bounded mapper's tiny
conditional pseudorandom bias.

## Identity arithmetic

The current source recipes use IdentityK=1/10, D=1000000 and ties-to-even at each
historical contribution in order. Each accepted e is nonzero and in[-1,1]. At most64
entries therefore yield nonnegative derived Support/Opposition with their sum≤64:
each lattice step adds at most1 to exactly one counter. Identity strength stays
strictly between-1 and1, never clipped. Nonzero contributions rounding to no increment
remain retained to preserve history and duplicate prevention.

All calculations are finite exact rationals. The source forecast has support≤64 and
each diagnostic reading lies on its existing tenth-unit lattice. A reduced mean has
denominator at most640. Task constants and the fixed calibration make concern,
unfloored meaning, alignment, fair win probabilities and authorship finite exact
expressions. This bounds work without rounding them to a new psychological lattice.
Only the explicitly named identity accumulation step quantizes.

The source read and application must reproduce the same ordered fold. Moving all
rounding to the end or replacing it with independent rounding of increments is not
licensed: at half ties, prior lattice parity can matter. Retained raw contributions
allow replay to check that exact history rather than trusting a cached scalar.

## Limits of the proposal

The finite recipe cohort still needs a machine materialization audit and actual
runtime controls. The older maxWork100 parameter is not carried forward as proof of
these bounds. The new27 is not frozen merely by appearing here. Larger times, open
calibration, more actors/tasks, physical source coexistence or longer retained history
require a separately reviewed successor. No new record, namespace or permanent member
number is assigned by this numeric-domain proposal.
