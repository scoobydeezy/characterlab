## Two different meanings of recomputation
Refolding the original frozen qualifications is a legitimate representation candidate.
Reinterpreting old choices using present contextual meaning is a different operation:
it changes what the character was choosing between at the time. The experiment keeps
both candidates so a future reduction cannot cite one as proof for the other.
No present context, learning or identity change may rewrite baseline old expressions.

DisplayOnly and HistoryOnly deliberately share decision behavior. Their diagnostic
display differs; both lack the feedback edge. No psychological distinction is invented
merely to force those two controls to disagree on action. An authored constant may
produce a modifier but cannot explain divergence from identical authored state.
