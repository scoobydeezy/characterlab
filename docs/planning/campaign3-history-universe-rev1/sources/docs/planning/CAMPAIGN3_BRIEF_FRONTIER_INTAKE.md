## Decision
Start with **agency, interference and attribution**, then **goals and strategy
persistence**. The first tests whether downstream learning preserves who chose,
who acted, who prevented execution and what each observer could know. Confusing these
can invalidate identity, social belief, controllability, relationship and strategy
learning together. Goals remain a major missing owner; taking them second avoids
building strategy adaptation on an untested account of failure and causal credit.

This is a local research sequence, not Campaign4's general reduction ranking. The
accepted Architecture explicitly distinguishes intent, execution, qualification and
observer-relative evidence. No new psychological edge or final learning law is chosen.
Ease of implementation and number of missing fixtures are not priority criteria.
