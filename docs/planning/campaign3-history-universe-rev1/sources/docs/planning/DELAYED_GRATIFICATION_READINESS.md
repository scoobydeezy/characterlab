# Delayed gratification - 2026-09-25

LOCAL DISPOSITION. Stage A intake; no allocation or qualification.
Next target: Brief12.7 clause6, currently BLOCKED. No owner ruling presumed.

The procrastination successor compares current work and immediate activity while
retaining an intended goal. It does not qualify delayed gratification: a promised
later opportunity is not an achieved later benefit, and postponing work is not the
same phenomenon as waiting for a desired outcome.

Audit Architecture11/12, BODY and commitment/goal lifecycle, reason/dice roles,
reference MEC012..020 and P3-012 before selecting a bounded source. Separate an
admitted offer/prediction, anticipated benefit, actual wait/choice, elapsed time,
physical delivery, received outcome and later learning. A future promise must not
be privileged truth or automatically become a new Need. No character consumer may
inspect unrevealed future delivery or secretly know that waiting will succeed.

Require an actual choice between an available immediate outcome and an expected
later outcome, followed by genuine waiting/delivery or failed delivery. Hold the
relevant benefit identity/value basis fixed when manipulating delay. Preserve
unknown, unavailable and uncertain later opportunities. Include false promise,
missing feedback, changed waiting cost and a serious no-temporal-discount comparator;
retain a competing discount law if the source domain can distinguish it. Do not
canonize a discount curve, universal patience trait or cross-domain exchange rate.

Use existing exact independent-ground reason/dice arbitration and causal delivery
boundaries. Waiting must not itself manufacture corroborating evidence, and received
reward must not retroactively alter the prior reason/expression. Remember the
procrastination finding: one available later opportunity does not imply enough
capacity or time to realize a larger goal. Bound or explicitly model that gap.
Resolve conservative finite choices locally; escalate only a genuine architectural
ontology/authority/new-edge issue. RO-C3-010/012/014/019/020 retain broader learning,
prospection, lifecycle and receiving limits.021 historical reconciliation remains
mandatory before Campaign3 exit.
