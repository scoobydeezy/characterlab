# Development: owner-directed learning and personality formation

**Owner directive,2026-10-01. Requirements accepted; implementation and qualification
remain OPEN.** Counters1492/0. No new verdict or allocation. This supersedes the
scope/order of DEVELOPMENT_STATE_READINESS.md; its frozen historical bytes remain.
In particular, developmental learning rates are now IN SCOPE. A direct current
disposition modifier alone cannot satisfy this work order.

The owner requires two independently manipulable developmental effects:

1. Higher plasticity in younger characters: equivalent admitted learning or actual
   practice can produce larger acquired changes, making new learning easier.
2. Substantially greater personality-forming influence of events experienced while
   young: equivalent eligible experience can contribute more to enduring personality.

These are capability requirements, not an empirical age curve or a selected formula.
Do not use one global multiplier that makes their independence untestable. Keep
constitutional starting parameters, developmental state, learned competence/belief,
identity eligibility, historical authorship and acquired personality distinct.

LOCAL DISPOSITION: represent the second effect as developmental personality-forming
weight, separate from the established authorship/eligibility judgment. For authored
expressions, first determine eligibility through the existing meaningfulness,
voluntariness/coercion and evidence rules; only eligible contributions receive the
developmental weight. Zero eligibility remains zero. Youth must not manufacture
agency, turn forced action into voluntary identity evidence, or rewrite a roll or
frozen DecisionExpression. Other experienced events may influence personality via
their accepted learning/adaptation routes; they must not be relabeled as the
character's authored choices merely because the character experienced them.

Use developmental state at the causal learning/formation event and preserve that
provenance. Later ageing must not retroactively weaken an earlier contribution by
recomputing its original weight from current age. Separately qualified forgetting,
revision or recovery can still occur; high early weight is not immutable destiny.
Older characters must remain capable of learning and personality change in the
bounded candidate; neither age nor plasticity grants hidden-truth access. Faster
learning from misleading admitted evidence may also produce faster mistaken learning.

## Required comparison and integration gates

- Match initial constitution, acquired state, exposure, admitted evidence, practice,
  goals and opportunities; intervene on developmental state only. Demonstrate a
  learning-rate difference and later behavior/competence, not just a changed rate field.
- Hold learning-rate influence fixed and vary personality-forming weight; hold that
  weight fixed and vary learning plasticity. Trace both paths to avoid accidentally
  counting one developmental effect twice.
- Compare early versus late equivalent eligible events, then bring characters to
  the same current developmental state and probe enduring personality influence.
  Current age alone must not explain away the retained historical difference.
- Include developmental gains disabled separately/together, constant-young and
  constant-mature controls, at least two age-trajectory/gain candidates, and equal
  sampled outcomes alongside discriminating ones. Select numerical bounds locally.
- Preserve trivial/no-evidence/coerced/forced-action exclusions, absent opportunity,
  false evidence, later corrective experience, saturation and old-age learning.
  More weight does not automatically mean stronger fear, better skill or stronger
  reasons everywhere. Require an explicit typed consumer for each claimed effect.
- Freeze a component contract and comparisons before execution; then close native
  developmental-source ownership, original lineage, phase ordering, save/restore,
  fault and publication gates. Do not amend predecessor frozen laws or receipts.

No calendar ages, biological units, domain-universal plasticity factor, critical
period, clinical maturation claim or irreversible personality lock is selected.
The authorizes-two-effects requirement resolves their inclusion; exact laws remain
research candidates. No owner ruling is currently pending. RO008/009/010/013/017/
