## What changed
An observed routine episode first supports exact recall. An explicit event-sampled
retention candidate later removes its peripheral detail from character memory while
preserving its identifying fragment. Recollection may then fill the missing detail
from an acquired category regularity, with a distinct Reconstructed tag. The guess
can contradict both the original observation and world truth.

Original observation, surviving imprint, acquired summary and present recollection
remain separately traceable. The baseline never retrieves lost detail from the
research archive. Repeated recall adds no summary credit and does not turn a guess
into remembered fact. New admitted observations can change later reconstruction
without rewriting the target fragment or earlier recollections.
