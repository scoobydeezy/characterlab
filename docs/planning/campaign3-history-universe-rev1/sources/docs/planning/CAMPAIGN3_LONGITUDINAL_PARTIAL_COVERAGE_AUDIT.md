## Scope of the requirement

Brief12.15 says long-run scenarios should eventually combine its listed ingredients
and become biographically specific. An isolated family witness is useful but does
not alone discharge the longitudinal integration obligation. This audit does not
require every ingredient in one first experiment, a lifetime simulation, a new root
per ingredient, or a universal psychological law. A bounded successor can qualify
one missing ingredient by integrating it with acquired biography and a meaningful
history/retention intervention. Existing qualified clauses remain unchanged.

LONG-001 has15 models/32 public runs/513 prefixes across biography, skill and
relationship, including a gap that expires recent episodes, skill interference and
relearning. It retains actual identity influence after acquisition. Its controlled
schedule is not itself a character routine; its instructions are not adopted goals;
skill practice is not acquired habitual availability; episode expiry is not personal
loss. FullHistory/CompactRelationship, EpisodicOnly, DestructiveSharedSlot, KeepAll,
NoLearning and the rust candidates remain preserved.

## Routines:12.15-2 remains PARTIAL

Repeated source opportunities in LONG, HABIT or biology do not establish that the
character maintains or resumes a routine. Biology's actual meal/drink/rest choices
are bodily regulation witnesses, not automatically a temporally organized routine.
No distinct Routine object is demanded. A derived policy may suffice if it predicts
routine continuation, interruption and resumption from the character's admitted
history/context rather than a scheduler label.

Next: acquire a bounded repeated practice, interrupt its normal opportunity, then
restore opportunity and measure whether/why it resumes while retained biography
remains available. Compare scheduled-only repetition, learned cue availability and
goal-supported organization; preserve cases where these are behaviorally equivalent.
Do not call every repeated action a routine or select a new mechanism in advance.

## Goals:12.15-6 remains PARTIAL

GOAL-001 supplies actual adoption, persistence through route replacement/gaps and
evidence-relative fulfillment (4 models/19 public runs/94 prefixes). CONTROL and
biology compose maintained goals with learned history and actual action. Defining
memory adds goal-relative historical significance, changed current interpretation,
retention and recall (68 programs/1680 prefixes). These later witnesses materially
narrow the gap; goals are implemented, not absent.

What is missing here is the joined longitudinal witness: an adopted goal's lifecycle
across acquired autobiographical standing, retention/interference and a later choice
or memory use, with the old meaning and later goal kept separate. Defining's sparse
idle ages and carried rehearsal cue do not establish that broader biographical use.
Compare maintained/replaced/retired goals under the same acquired history, plus
GoalEqualsPlan and NoFeedback where applicable. Do not duplicate existing goal owners
or conflate a task instruction with an adopted desired outcome.

## Habits:12.15-8 remains PARTIAL

HABIT-001's12 models/23 public runs/291 prefixes prove acquired cue-dependent
availability after explicit expectation correction; CONTROL adds actual inhibition,
load-triggered action and renewed inhibition without erasure. Native biology further
combines learned access, beliefs, goals, actual action, physiological adaptation and
recovery (28 models/58 distinct runs/270 unique prefixes). These are real temporal
compositions, not merely a list of independent APIs.

They do not yet test acquired habit together with the longitudinal biography and
episodic-retention intervention. Original HABIT fixes identity/body contributions and
competence; biology's first public profile does not acquire identity. Later native
identity supplies a separate biological expression channel, but that does not
establish this retention/habit comparison. Reuse those implementations in a bounded
successor: acquire both history and biography, remove recent episodic detail, test
cue response and goal inhibition, then relearning. Keep NoHistory/ExplicitBeliefOnly,
EraseHistory, alternate cue and stored/derived comparisons. Do not infer equivalence
between an admitted habit journal and general episodic memory.

## Loss:12.15-11 remains PARTIAL

GRIEF-001 (7 models/31 public runs/279 prefixes) combines actual acquired support
history with fallible future-contact evidence, separation, practical substitutes and
later revision. Enduring loss differs from temporary absence and from utility loss;
prospective response distributions change. This already qualifies bounded grief
after believed loss, and must not be described as an unimplemented capability.

The remaining longitudinal comparison is subsequent biographical behavior across
loss and retained/recalled history. GRIEF ends at prospective distributions, not
sampled mourning/search or enduring adjustment; LONG's episode expiry and skill
interference do not substitute for personal loss. Integrate a believed-loss source
after acquired history, compare temporary/unknown/enduring return evidence under
matched absence, then a later meaningful choice and corrective evidence. Preserve
original relationship/identity meaning and distinguish belief revision from history
erasure. Retain TemporaryAbsenceOnly, CurrentUtilityOnly, NoHistory and TruthOracle;
do not require a physical death model or a clinical grief progression.
