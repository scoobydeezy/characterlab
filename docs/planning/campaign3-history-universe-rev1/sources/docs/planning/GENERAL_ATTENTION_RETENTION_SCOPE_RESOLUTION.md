# Ordinary retention: bounded per-kind budgets accepted

Preserve that packet as history. This acceptance settles resource topology, not an

## One lifecycle authority, non-borrowing partitions

The first ordinary-memory model gives event/continuant and interoceptive acquisitions
separate bounded retention domains under one ordinary-memory lifecycle authority.
The partitions do not borrow capacity from each other. A new body acquisition cannot
directly evict an event/continuant acquisition, or conversely. Same-kind pressure
must still have a declared memory consequence.

Both kinds share AcquisitionOccurrenceId addressing, ordinary read authorization,
history/persistence, architectural consolidation services, pruning ownership and
replay/idempotency discipline. Typed policies and payloads may differ. This does not
authorize separate memory universes, private remembered-sample stores or independent
writers replacing the same old family leaf.

Partitioning is a first-model hypothesis, not an invariant of biological memory.
Shared cross-kind competition remains a named future comparator; it requires an
explicit common accounting, survival and fragmentation law before implementation.
No common importance scalar or exchange rate follows from the shared identity or
authority. Acquisition count, byte footprint and psychological importance are not
interchangeable quantities.

## Actual forgetting is required

Each kind must exhibit genuine retention pressure from valid admitted acquisitions.
A full cognitive pool invokes the declared retention/fragmentation/pruning behavior;
it cannot merely reject the run or retain everything until the fixture ends.
Source/work safety limits remain separately enforced admission bounds. Exceeding
such a safety limit is not evidence of modeled forgetting.

Current accessibility is excluded from the first eviction policy. Retained but
currently inaccessible memory must remain possible. MEC-010's accessibility,
recency/frequency and retrieval-reinforcement controls do not become an eviction
formula by reuse. A later retention hypothesis using long-term access history needs
its own qualified meaning; no such hypothesis is accepted here.

Age, reinforcement history and consolidation status may be investigated as typed
retention inputs, but this ruling chooses neither their equation nor their priority.
It does not authorize FIFO, lowest-strength deletion, largest-payload deletion or
an authored defining-memory flag. The next within-kind policy must expose its actual
evidence basis and preserve the retention/accessibility distinction.

## Consolidation cannot evade resource ownership

A representation produced by consolidation needs an explicitly governed bounded
retention class. Pruning body evidence and copying it into an unlimited background
dictionary is prohibited. Any future representation crossing acquisition kinds
requires an explicit resource-ownership decision; ownership is not inferred from
the originating partition or the word consolidation.

Initial acquisition history remains immutable under lifecycle B. Character-accessible
surviving content may change only through its declared retention/consolidation rules.
Trace history is not a retrieval archive. Acquisition identities are not reused, and
source IDs do not restore discarded samples. Bounded replay/idempotency bookkeeping
must not preserve an undeclared copy of forgotten content.

## Frozen public obligations, not passed

1. Valid event/continuant acquisitions exceed their cognitive pool and produce an
   observable declared memory loss/change; the analogous body witness also executes.
2. With event memory and its retention operands fixed, additional body pressure does
   not evict it; the reciprocal cross-kind control also holds. No spare-slot borrowing.
3. A retained acquisition can be inaccessible under one cue/context; changing current
   accessibility alone does not change retention or trigger eviction.
4. Safety-bound rejection and valid cognitive-capacity pressure are distinct outcomes.
5. Later reads expose surviving admitted content only, never pruned sample bytes via
   source, selection, acquisition, trace or background-state lookup.
6. Same-instant formations and retention losses reconcile under one family authority,
   with complete rollback, replay and persistent continuation at exact bounded state.
7. Consolidated representations obey their declared resource class and bound; no
   cross-kind or unbounded storage escape is possible.

These are accepted obligations, not results from the earlier six scheduler fixtures.
Those tests exercise append-only fixture state and do not qualify forgetting, partition
enforcement, public memory admission or persistence after pruning.
