## Semantic direction selected locally

Preserve the adopted commitment's motive-generating Base and its empty observational
basis. A new, explicit task criterion can interpret permitted body evidence as
Situation for that commitment. The body source independently produces its Base
from its own accepted criterion. Both may cite one actual CharacterEvidenceRef.
Neither copies the other's raw signal, pressure or reason nucleus.

The first baseline retains candidate/ground/role/sign partitioning. Compare an
explicit source-normalization candidate only after its active-ground and role scope
is fully declared. The role-blind FamilyNormalized0.1 comparison remains an immutable
counterexample; it is not the proposed public law. GroundPairwise and DescriptionDice
remain named negative controls with their exact applicable domains.

## Producer obligations

1. **Adopted criterion:** bind an active, selected task/commitment, holder, complete
   option, body-signal domain and exact criterion. It must distinguish the task's
   criterion from the body pressure definition. Registry presence is not adoption.
2. **Permitted evidence:** consume an actual accepted observation or read-only body
   recollection with the correct observer, holder, schema, source version and time.
   Missing evidence is unavailable; it is not zero discrepancy. Explicitly choose
   the observation-only or retained-history first profile before shape acceptance.
3. **Own interpretation:** produce task-specific situational evidence without
   converting the body interval into the diagnostic forecast361, inventing a mean,
   reading hidden reserves or claiming expected action efficacy. Keep interval
   uncertainty and exact units. A conservative threshold interpretation based on
   the observed upper bound has existing body arithmetic precedent, but its task
   meaning, criterion direction and range must be accepted separately.
4. **Common support:** carry the same actual observer-safe reference into both
   independently produced signals for the shared-fact control. Never equate hidden
   truth handles, channel-name similarity, ordinal magnitude or dispatcher ancestry
   with character evidence equality.
5. **Independent support:** admit two independently controlled source operands,
   such as distinct local-reserve signal domains already expressible in GA, with
   explicit domain/subject binding and independently varied source histories. Two
   observations of one quantity are not an independent-fact proof merely because
   their IDs differ. Equal output magnitudes are permitted as a matched control;
   their evidence must still come from the real producers.
6. **Composition:** same complete option, separate ground and family, exact shared
   source join, sole inherited state owners and data-only originals. No direct raw
   signal ingress, fake TaskKey for body, hidden caller-supplied coverage or learned
   efficacy. No new persistent Need or appraisal state is needed for this profile.

## Deliberately unfinished design fields

These are local contract-design tasks, not requests for an architecture-owner ruling:

- Choose the actual accepted body evidence schema/source and the minimal two-domain
  original calendar. The old single-reserve461 profile cannot be expanded by a flag.
- Fix task criterion semantics, uncertainty treatment, exact response law and sign
  intervention. A negative deficit signal is currently only a numerical control;
  a public negative contribution needs a legitimate task interpretation.
- Specify authenticated sharing/independent-source joins, read domains, subject
  projection, output grammar and existing/required occurrence kinds.
- Declare active-ground/role/sign normalization scope and the third aggregate basis
  producer. Preserve the possibility of a component-only third-basis exclusion;
  do not call PHEN-MULTI whole PASS while its public obligation is missing.
- Freeze exact matched inputs, comparison observations and coupling before any
  model images. Admit source loss, duplicate descriptions, altered sign, shared versus
  independent facts, no-base modifier, permuted source order and hidden-truth controls.

No numeric ID is reserved here. After semantic/shape acceptance, separately review
the smallest allocation, implement actual producers and distinct model compilers,
then execute public comparisons, rollback, replay and preservation. Successful
component arithmetic does not discharge those gates.
