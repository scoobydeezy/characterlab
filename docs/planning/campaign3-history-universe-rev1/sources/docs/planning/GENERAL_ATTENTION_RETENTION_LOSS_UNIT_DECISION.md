# First retention loss unit and scarcity measure

2026-09-12. **CONCRETE ARCHITECTURAL DECISION REQUIRED.** Follows the
[substrate review](ATTENTION_RETENTION_SUBSTRATE_REVIEW_REV1.md). Non-borrowing per-kind
budgets and one ordinary-memory authority remain accepted and are not reopened.

## Choice

**A — whole-acquisition retirement first.** A kind's cognitive capacity counts
individuated acquisitions. Pressure retires one or more complete acquisitions under
an explicitly reviewed within-kind policy. This is genuine forgetting, and the
smallest initial loss mechanism. It supplies no partial-content survival witness;
fragmentation remains an additional North Star lifecycle obligation before claiming
that broader behavior. Do not present A as a complete memory-lifecycle reduction.

**B — complete retained-unit fragmentation first (recommended).** A kind's cognitive
capacity counts surviving retained child units, with separate finite safety limits
on acquisitions, bytes and work. Event/continuant units use their existing semantic
unit key; body units are whole signal groups, including all of a group's retained
views. Pressure removes complete units according to a separately declared typed
retention policy. A nonempty remainder keeps its AcquisitionOccurrenceId. Loss of
the last unit removes the current individual recall target; history remains governed
and no acquisition identity is reused.

Within the body partition, one multi-view signal group consumes one cognitive unit;
its views remain subject to explicit separate safety bounds. This is a retention
scarcity hypothesis, not automatic reuse of the acquisition gate's unit choice.
There is no cross-kind exchange rate because the partitions do not borrow capacity.
No synthetic cue, inferred summary, numeric estimate or new learned pattern is created
merely because some content is removed.

B is recommended because it can witness a surviving core with a lost peripheral
member, rather than requiring all-or-nothing recall. It fits the accepted stable
group addresses and preserves exact evidence bundles without inventing fragment IDs.
It costs a survivor-content state/read contract and auxiliary-state reconciliation.
Whole-acquisition retirement remains a named simpler comparator, not a rejected model.

## Distinguishing example

An interoceptive acquisition A contains two selected groups, alpha and beta. Alpha
has two admitted interval views; beta has one. Later same-kind pressure occurs.

Under A, retiring acquisition A removes both signals. Under B, a declared policy may
remove beta while preserving alpha, its two exact views and acquisition A's identity.
Later authorized recall can distinguish those outcomes. Selecting alpha as survivor
in this example is illustrative, not an accepted importance law or tie break.
The corresponding event case preserves one encoded unit while another becomes
unavailable; it cannot recover the omitted unit from an old selected envelope.

Keeping only one of alpha's views is deliberately outside proposed B. Doing so could
change the character's evidence basis in ways requiring a separately reviewed
within-group forgetting model. Equal signal values still do not merge identities.

## Guardrails independent of the choice

Neither alternative permits accessibility-ranked eviction, hidden reserve-based
priority, guessed body importance, unbounded background storage, cross-kind borrowing
or run rejection merely because cognitive capacity is full. Actual acquired importance
and the within-kind survival law remain explicit next work; this decision does not
turn encoding strength or Supported attribution into that law.

Initial committed encoding bytes remain historical. Current memory contains only
survivors; history and producer identifiers grant no access to removed evidence.
Partial survival creates no new acquisition. Same-instant admissions/losses reconcile
under one authority, with bounded metadata, complete rollback and qualified replay.
Persistent graph/presentation consequences must be declared rather than silently
dropping learned state or keeping an unbounded reference to removed content.

No numeric capacity, schema allocation or canonical implementation is proposed by
this packet. The user ruling selects the first loss operation and its accounting
domain; exact policy, source-derived operands and public proofs follow.
