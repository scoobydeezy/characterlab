# Attribution-gated consolidation: accepted direction

2026-09-12. **Credit-assignment subdecision ACCEPTED.** This records the planning
analysis supplied by the user after the credit decision request. Lifecycle B is

The first authorized outcome-linked consolidation path is attribution-gated:

    experienced outcome + permitted episode/outcome connection evidence
      → separately traceable observer-side attribution assessment
      → eligibility for outcome-dependent consolidation credit

The [original decision request](GENERAL_ATTENTION_CONSOLIDATION_CREDIT_DECISION.md)

## Frozen architectural constraints

The assessment must be explicit and inspectable. Conscious propositional belief,
natural-language reasoning, a general causal graph and a stored causal belief are
not prerequisites imposed by this ruling. OutcomeAttributionAssessment is a useful
provisional name, not an allocated schema or new belief family.

Attribution may be uncertain or objectively wrong. Its actual operands must be
permitted character evidence. Temporal proximity, common subject and hidden
authoritative effect provenance are insufficient by themselves. The character's
conclusion must remain invariant under changes in hidden causal truth that preserve
all admitted operands. Runtime admission can authenticate an evidence producer;
that authentication does not establish the truth of the resulting attribution.

Supported attribution makes this path eligible. Insufficient evidence or no observed
outcome makes credit unavailable. Neither means effect=0, known absence of causation,
nor supported contrary attribution. Contrary-attribution response and uncertainty
calibration require their own exact semantics; no numeric confidence is implied here.

Eligibility is not an effect measurement and does not by itself authorize a state
write. A subsequent consolidation assessment must have independently justified
operands and a qualified law. The existing observer-safe-bin counterexamples remain
binding: attribution cannot recover hidden Applied, Potential or Overflow.

Attribution and any later correction append cognitive history. They do not rewrite
the original episode, initial encoding factors or previous assessment, and do not
backdate a Cause role into the initial SEM envelope. The target must already be
encoded, or be a valid base in the explicitly declared atomic formation composition
under lifecycle B. The same-instant composition must not resurrect excluded content.

## Temporal association remains a future candidate

Experiential association may later coexist with causal-credit consolidation. It is
not rejected, merged, retired or qualified by this ruling. A later association seam
may assert co-occurrence/contextual association under bounded eligibility; it may
not silently populate Cause, event-to-outcome attribution or Need relevance because
an event produced an outcome. Its possible effects on accessibility, valence or
anticipation remain hypotheses, not authorized persistent writes.

MEC-007/CTL-004 retain the causal-relevance control; MEC-008/009/010 retain distinct
association/access/reinforcement controls. No historical mechanism is silently lost.
The minimal EVID269/270 contract, SEM causal-role domain and character evidence
reference union remain unchanged. New assessment admission requires a new exact
seam rather than an indirect truth handle or generic payload resolver.

## Accepted proof obligations — frozen, not passed

1. A merely recent episode with no admitted connection evidence gets no causal credit.
2. Supported false attribution is possible: hidden truth does not veto it.
3. Equal permitted operands produce equal assessment at matched output identities.
4. Foreign observer, foreign subject and unencoded target admission reject.
5. Insufficient support and an unobserved outcome yield unavailable credit, not zero
   effect or a claim of no connection.
6. Original episode bytes and earlier attribution records remain immutable.
7. Removing attribution support isolates the downstream consolidation effect while
   holding episode, outcome, timing and bodily evidence fixed.

The13 earlier component/fixture tests remain evidence for scheduler expressibility
and source limitations only. They do not pass these public attribution obligations.

no reapproval of this decision is needed.
