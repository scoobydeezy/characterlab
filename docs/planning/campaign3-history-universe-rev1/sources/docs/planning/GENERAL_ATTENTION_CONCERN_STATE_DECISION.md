# What is the first bodily concern?

2026-09-12. Historical packet; [A subsequently accepted](GENERAL_ATTENTION_MAINTENANCE_GOAL_RESOLUTION.md).
Concern-relative observed consequences are accepted.
This decision chooses the actual psychological state that can supply the concern.

## A — adopted bodily maintenance goal (recommended bounded first)

Use a separately governed prospective goal: this character is currently committed
to maintaining an admitted perceived bodily signal in a desired interval. It can be
withdrawn or expire; satisfaction does not automatically retire a maintenance goal.
Two characters with identical bodies can hold different goals. Appraisal consumes
the goal at consequence time; historical assessments survive later goal changes.

This uses the architecture's goals/prospection owner, without treating the existing
TaskConcern output as a goal. Existing one-shot measurement-task records cannot be
widened or reused unchanged: their target domain and retirement rule differ.

## B — durable bodily preference

Make the desired condition a standing value/preference rather than an active goal.
It may matter even when no explicit maintenance commitment is active. This needs
a separately governed preference basis, identity, acquisition/revision and state owner.
It cannot be manufactured from pressure or a constant called importance.

## Why this changes behavior

Hold body, consequence and perceived evidence constant. After the character withdraws
an active maintenance goal, A no longer supplies that goal's assessment basis.
A durable preference in B may still supply one. Neither permits rewriting earlier
assessments. Choosing by convenience would decide whether concern depends on an
active commitment or persists beyond it.

[The concrete draft](CONCERN_STATE_AND_ASSESSMENT_DRAFT_REV1.md) provides the A state
inventory, an explicitly unaccepted satisfaction-boundary assessment proposal,
worked counterexamples and adversarial obligations. The proposed law detects
attainment/violation rather than measuring physical magnitude. It exposes important
limitations; it is not frozen merely by choosing the concern owner.

Requested ruling: authorize A as the first concern-state source, or B if the first
experiment should model a standing preference without an active goal. Numeric law,
credit granularity and survival composition remain separate unresolved choices.
