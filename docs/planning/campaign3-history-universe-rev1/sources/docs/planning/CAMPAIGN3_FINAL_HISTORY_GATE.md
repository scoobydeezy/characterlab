1. **Define the universe before reconciliation.** Commit deterministic inclusion
   rules and an enumerable manifest covering verdicts, accepted decisions,
   implementation findings, qualification/closure reports, and active/reference
   mechanism ledgers. Include canonical historical research findings referenced by
   those authorities. Record paths, versions/hashes, authority and acceptance or
   supersession evidence. Filename patterns alone cannot establish accepted status.
   Account explicitly for excluded/superseded candidates and unresolved references;
   an inconvenient unresolved result is not a reason for exclusion.
2. **Extract an occurrence inventory independently of the RO registry.** Locate
   unresolved, retracted, derived and blocked markers, including the project's
   equivalent labels (PARTIAL, DEFERRED, UNTESTED, NOT REQUIRED, COMPRESSED, RETIRED,
   reopen/limitation statements). Preserve source location, exact context and a
   stable occurrence identity. Record extraction rules and review prose findings
   that keyword matching could miss. Mechanical coverage proves enumeration under
   those rules, not semantic completeness by itself.
3. **Reconcile every occurrence.** Link each material unresolved distinction to
   an owned RO with blocked claims and closure/reopen conditions. For resolved,
   superseded, retracted or historical-only markers, cite the exact later evidence
   and scope rather than silently clearing the label. DERIVED/NOT REQUIRED results
   need the preserved semantics, representation assumptions, information loss,
   future query set and horizon. Non-findings/quoted markers need an explicit
   reviewed exclusion rationale. Many occurrences may cite the same finding;
   deduplication must preserve every source occurrence.
4. **Check completeness and freshness.** Fail on missing artifacts, changed hashes,
   omitted occurrences, orphan dispositions, broken RO/evidence links, unexplained
   exclusions or unreviewed additions to the canonical set. Record the enumeration,
   reconciliation and checks as a versioned receipt. Independently review the
   universe's adequacy and the scientific dispositions before claiming zero
   unreconciled material findings within that universe.
5. **Keep coverage separate.** Reconciled conditional debt is not a passed corpus
   clause. The unchanged 21-member corpus and all fifteen Brief families still need
   their own exit dispositions under the governing authority hierarchy. General
   Campaign4 ranking stays withheld until the campaign exit requirements are met.

The final pass must preserve failed cohorts and correct-forward history. It must
not rewrite earlier receipts to suggest later source bytes or broader qualification.
The readiness audit's source snapshot and its metadata checker are inputs to this
gate, not substitutes for its final universe and reconciliation proof.
