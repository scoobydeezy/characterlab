# Concentrated dependence and alternative satisfiers — 2026-09-26

LOCAL DISPOSITION. Stage A intake for Brief12.9 clauses4/5; both remain BLOCKED.
No allocation, model, new qualification or owner ruling. Start with Architecture
body/need, learning, habit, goal and reason ownership; North Star dependence/substitution;
REFERENCE_MECHANISM_LEDGER EXP001/003/004/009 and applicable MEC011..020.
Inspect BODY, ATTACHMENT, HABIT and CONTROL contracts and frozen evidence first.

ATTACHMENT already distinguishes perceived dependence history, expected relief,
current practical utility and missing-contact appraisal. Its alternative is a
controlled adequate-alternative Boolean and its action probes are prospective;
it does not establish actual alternative-satisfier acquisition, choice and delivery.
HABIT already demonstrates acquired cue availability independent of current expected
reward, but its one target plus idle cannot establish healthy substitution among
multiple satisfiers. Do not rename either result into whole dependence coverage.

A bounded successor should independently vary learned success with one satisfier
versus multiple viable satisfiers, current admitted availability, current demand and
cue/history pressure. Keep expected relief separate from acquired habit, actual
physical relief separate from admitted feedback, and lack of opportunity separate
from observed failure. Use a common current state where possible so the intervention
isolates history concentration rather than unequal total reward or current need.
Require actual reasons/arbitration/intent/execution and later learning: does removal
of the familiar satisfier permit use of a learned available substitute? What changes
when that substitute is unknown, believed ineffective or unavailable? Preserve
unknown versus known failure and false/missing evidence.

Before implementation, accept an exact versioned bounded contract with provenance,
source timing, state ownership, competitor identities and public/component scope.
Reuse existing belief/history where sufficient; do not author a Dependence or Addicted
trait. Compare at least current-expectation-only and history-sensitive candidates;
keep equal current expectations with different history when claiming history effects.
A fixed baseline is a candidate, not a general reinforcement/resource law.
Escalate only if the accepted architecture cannot determine a required new boundary
or causal edge. Finite source calibration and comparator differences remain local.

No tolerance, withdrawal, craving, escalating reinforcement, relapse or general
addiction claim follows. Those separate clauses stay in the denominator. RO008/010/
012/015/019/020 preserve relevant source, learning, control and integration limits;
RO021 remains the mandatory final historical gate. No reduction is proposed.
