## Recommended research target

Investigate a thin episodic measurement memory: a character can retain the fact that they
observed a particular measurement on a particular occasion and later recall that measurement
through an authorized memory consumer. This is a proposal to remember an observation, with
its observer-relative scope, not to assert the present or hidden regulatory state.

The positive result must include a later usable recollection, not merely another wrapper,
archive entry or changed state digest. Proposed ADAPT-9b witness: matched exposure and later
permitted probe produce 5 versus 51/10 at the same evidence identities/topology; before that
observation all epistemic state is equal; subsequent governed memory formation differs only
because of admitted content; a later identical recall opportunity yields the corresponding
remembered measurement after the transient intake is over. Restore must preserve that result.
Selecting this target would authorize inspection and drafting, not establish this witness as
qualified or close any parent gate.

## Alternative and decision

A measurement-conditioned belief/estimate could instead be the first responding mechanism.
That requires selecting a proposition domain, time interpretation, uncertainty semantics and
revision rule before mathematics; the observed measurement cannot silently become an estimate
of hidden D or R0. It is a larger semantic commitment than retaining an observation.

Recommendation: select episodic measurement retention plus later recall for inspection-first
formalization. The user must choose the research target because carriage's acceptance selected
no memory or belief mechanism. Defer record shapes, permanent numbers, equations, packaging,
implementation, general retrieval, appraisal/reward, expectation learning and all gate closure
until the relevant decisions and proofs exist.
