## Why the boundary recipes exist

and prediction read40 at2, one task expires at2, and the remaining live task generates
a settled choice and its complete attempt/qualification/application branch. The
qualifier may reject zero authorship, but its application event remains mandatory.

history-boundary can generate64 genuine nonzero identity contributions without
exceeding the old adaptation bounds. An initial probe at1 supplies forecast0; repeated
deliberations at2..65 keep both distinct task criteria live. Blocked execution produces
count0, not a suppressed adaptation branch. The65th novel contribution at66 must fail
atomically at the history cap, preserving the committed64-entry history and all other
state, queue, output, trace, allocator and random-address state. These are proposed
controls, not reported passes. A test cannot inject a prefilled identity log into S0.

actions, another character, an open unit vocabulary or a new REG conversion. Broader
cohorts require a separately reviewed model/profile extension. These twenty-one recipes
are still proposals pending whole symbolic and numeric/model closure.
