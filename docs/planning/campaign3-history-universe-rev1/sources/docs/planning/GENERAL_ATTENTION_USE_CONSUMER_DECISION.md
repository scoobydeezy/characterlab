## A — retained-operand contrastive attribution (recommended first)

Authorize a bounded successor that constructs the existing contrastive assessment
from admitted surviving retained children and observer-safe outcome evidence. Freeze
exact roles and provenance per actually consumed child, preserving the accepted
assessment mathematics. Both Supported and Unavailable may be successfully committed
assessments: qualification follows actual operand use and commit, not result label.
Early exit cannot credit every supplied candidate merely because it was available;
the successor needs an explicit consumed-operand projection and adversarial controls.

First timing scope: use only previously committed surviving children. Complete and
commit the consumer result before the later retention instant that reads protection.
No new-formation transient access or same-instant result/retention coupling. This is
a proposed bounded scope, not a claim that cross-instant delay is a universal law.

This gives the smallest extension of an accepted cognitive job. It does not make
attribution Supported itself a protection signal, outcome benefit or importance.

## Required proof after choice

Bind real child reads and the actual committed result; reject fabricated receipts,
wrong subject, unconsumed siblings and failed transactions. A committed assessment
with an unfavorable or unavailable answer must not be equated with unsuccessful
operation. Preserve pure-recall exclusion and detect consumer-use removal. Prove
later retention uses only committed protection; lost children cannot be resurrected.
Public wrappers, allocations, write ownership and persistence remain separate gates.

Requested ruling: choose A with the bounded prior-committed-child/later-retention
scope, or B with a new prediction task. Neither choice closes the separate mandatory
outcome-derived significance challenge or all of General Attention.
