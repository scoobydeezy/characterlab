Four controlled demands concern one protective undertaking. Refuse means preserving
that undertaking (+1/2 alignment); comply means violating it (-1/2). This relation is
authored prior meaning, not inferred from punishment. The target is exactly the actor
owned by the existing biological-choice kernel; two distinct named external actors
own controlled conditional penalty policies. Demander recognition is admitted input.
External agents have no learned strategy or autonomous threat selection in this profile.

Each opportunity freezes prior target evidence for this demander, estimates refusal
cost, and compiles actual competing reasons through unmodified biological-choice/0.1.
Refusal has strength750 protective motive; compliance has strength250 request motive.
Admitted anticipated refusal cost adds a distinct compliance ground, not a chosen-action
override. Existing exact reason dice and addressed randomness select the action.
No demand means no decision, execution, penalty attempt or evidence.

The original expression is chosen action, actor, exact authorship and signed alignment.
Qualification occurs before execution. Threshold excludes credit for cost>=500;
Graded scales authorship-times-alignment by (1000-cost)/1000. IgnorePressure preserves
the actual pressure motive but omits its qualification gate. NoPressure disables the
receiver and gate while retaining learned evidence. NoLearning records observation
receipts but does not update retained evidence. Latest replaces the baseline floor of
the sample mean with the latest cost. All six are candidates, not settled laws.
Significance and undertaking acceptance are fixed high controls. Original exact signed
contributions fold through the existing millionth-quantized identity history with K=1.

external policy attempts a penalty only on an enacted refusal. Its power gates actual
resource subtraction (0,250 or1000 units from initial4000); no demand, compliance or
failed target execution provides a refusal trial. Accessible evidence records target,
recognized demander, time and displayed cost. Default display is actual loss; explicit
false displays remain controls. Withheld evidence is absence, never zero. A visible
refusal with no effective penalty can supply known zero. Observation precedes learning,
which affects only later choices. Historical expressions and credits never change.

Knowledge is source-indexed by target and demander; foreign sources cannot pool.
No learner or qualifier receives private power, planned penalty or later physical loss.
The target view omits physical execution/loss/attempt diagnostics. This is a controlled
receipt channel, not a general perception or causal attribution solution. Resource loss
does not additionally feed body/control/affect in this profile. No claim of a universal
coercion threshold, clinical trauma, legal voluntariness or general hierarchy is made.
