## Controlled source and retained experience

No recurrent concern mechanism is claimed or carried forward as fatigue. Original1334
contains CONTROL's six fields, DisplayMode0 authentic/1 rested/2 mild/3 severe,
FatigueVisible, FatigueRecipient, and PhysicalFatigue0 rested/1 mild/2 severe.
The only cognitive source is observation120: when visible and addressed to the
observer, grade = PhysicalFatigue for authentic display, else DisplayMode-1.
Absent/denied source gives empty optional grade, never known zero. A controlled
sensor/monitor establishes this one body-condition channel; ordinary interoception,
calibration and natural recognition are not qualified. Misleading reports are allowed.
The physical grade is an apparatus condition, not a caller-authored character belief.

Root1351 maps the sole character to1350, an initially empty ordered journal of

fatigue140. Latest grade is retained experienced condition; missing observations
neither restore capacity nor reinforce fatigue. Earlier observations are immutable.

No elapsed-time decay, inferred recovery, competence loss or new Need state.

## Independent control and execution candidates
Goal maintenance and the external neutral card are inherited unchanged. The two-slot
capacity remains: maintained goal uses one and inhibition needs the spare. Fatigue
is NOT another occupying card. It independently gates the effectiveness of an
otherwise available inhibitory operation. Workspace1337 stores maintained goal,
external card, control-impairment flag and whether fatigue is known. Evaluation1339
stores original appraisal/goal/load/inhibition plus retained experience, impairment,
known flag and grade (diagnostic0 when unknown, distinguished by the known flag).

1 SevereThreshold: known grade2 impairs inhibition; known0/1 and unknown do not.
2 NoFatigue: stores identical observations but fatigue has no control/execution effect.
3 MotorOnly: stores identical observations and retains normal cognitive control;
   executor110 alone reads physical grade and blocks performed target action at2.
   This is a serious execution-impairment explanation, not a cognitive truth read.
4 AnyFatigue: known grade1 or2 impairs inhibition, retaining threshold uncertainty.

For all: inhibited = free-probe AND maintained active goal AND no external card
AND NOT control-impaired. Lack of fatigue evidence gives no fatigue-specific penalty;
this conservative default does not claim knowledge of being rested. Learned habit,
reward belief and goal remain separate. Actual options50, reasons51/52, dice60,
intent70, expression80, plan90, attempt100 and execution110 are inherited.
MotorOnly can preserve intended exercise while failing execution on a forced exercise
opportunity. It never erases retained skill/habit merely because physical grade changes.
SevereThreshold/AnyFatigue deliberately test experienced-condition control modulation;
they do not claim to model all direct physiological impairment. NoFatigue and MotorOnly
remain controls, not rejected universal models.
