# Retained Phenomenon Corpus

**Status:** registry scaffold

The North Star and Research Program Brief currently describe the required phenomena in prose. Before the first campaign, each must receive a stable `PhenomenonId`, an observable obligation, necessary counterfactuals, epistemic constraints, time horizon, and pass/fail comparison rule.

The `EXP-*` and `P3-*` entries in the [Reference Mechanism Preservation Ledger](REFERENCE_MECHANISM_LEDGER.md) are the historical intake queue. They are not considered transferred until represented here with the complete fields below; this prevents old scenario code from masquerading as a phenomenon specification.

Minimum entry:

```text
PhenomenonId:
Version:
Name:
Required setup domain:
Intervention(s):
Observable obligation:
Causal counterfactual obligation:
Epistemic obligation:
Historical/developmental horizon:
Exact comparison rule:
Required trace fields:
Applicable seams:
Reopen conditions:
```

The corpus is the common exam for the intact architecture and every reduction. Scenarios are fixtures; phenomena are obligations. One phenomenon may require several fixtures, and one fixture may exercise several phenomena.
