## The distinction to make observable

The same predicted diagnostic reading can be relevant to one live goal, irrelevant
to another, or neutral with respect to an already satisfied desired interval. A
forecast has no valence by itself. A goal is not a Need meter, and neither concern
nor a goal directly selects an action.

The next bounded target is:

    retained prediction + concrete live commitment
        → bounded workspace selection
        → goal-relative forecast appraisal
        → transient affective conclusion
        → live motive activation

Each arrow remains a separate owned transition, even if the first deterministic
operation is small. Later option construction, raw signals, Reason Nuclei and
arbitration must consume these generated records, not hand-authored pressures.
This is the next portion of the Campaign2 topology, not its completion gate.

## Candidate semantics to formalize

One concrete commitment names a desired interval for the same PredictionDefinitionId
and one independently authored importance. It has a start, deadline and lifecycle;
the commitment identity is distinct from its definition, stakeholder and observed
subject. A live commitment may be initial authored character context, but its
workspace selection, appraisal, affect and motive must be computed in the causal
path. Initial context is not permission to preauthor a Reason or chosen intent.

For a selected live goal with desired interval[L,U] and known forecast m, a first
candidate appraisal may retain direction Below/Within/Above and exact distance
max(L-m,0,m-U). This is a **predicted goal discrepancy**, not a statement of current
truth, success probability, reward, blame or action controllability. The interval
uses the declared forecast domain; no OBS↔REG conversion is inferred.

The first affect candidate should be a separately typed transient concern derived
from that appraisal and declared importance. Its normalization, range, missing-input
behavior and future influence must be explicit, versioned mathematics. Do not call
the raw distance "fear" without the additional threat/control semantics fear needs.
No persistent affect field is earned merely by constructing a transient conclusion.

Motive generation must preserve the independent live commitment source. Predicted
within-range status is not actual fulfillment and cannot retire it. An inactive
commitment emits no base motive, even when past identity/affect remains. Concern may
later modify an already meaningful reason only through the separately registered
modifier contract; it does not supply an alternative motive implicitly.

## Frozen research comparisons before shape selection

1. Hold forecast fixed; change only desired interval. Appraisal changes with goal
   relevance, while observation, prediction and its historical support stay equal.
2. Hold goal fixed; change only permitted forecast through the actual prediction
   path. The discrepancy changes through that operand, not current REG/trace.
3. Absent forecast versus known within-range forecast must remain distinguishable;
   unknown is not asserted safety or zero discrepancy.
4. Remove/retire the concrete commitment while preserving identity and forecast.
   Its workspace/motive contribution disappears; no modifier resurrects it.
5. Reactivate a recurrence under a fresh instance ID. Its reason referent cannot
   collide with the retired instance, even for the same stakeholder/definition.
6. Change inaccessible truth while holding admitted forecast/context fixed. No
   cognitive operand changes. Any component/public split must be named.
7. Disable workspace access or appraisal/affect independently. Later consumers may
   not reconstruct missing operands from trace, REG or a cue's accidental payload.
8. Change affect while holding base motives/options fixed; the downstream decision
   must still arbitrate rather than obey an affect-to-action command. This final
   comparison cannot pass until the retained arbitration contract is ported.
9. Fulfillment, relinquishment, missed deadline and external cancellation must
   remain distinct history where implemented; absence of pressure alone does not
   prove the cause of retirement.

initial instance admission, active interval endpoints, terminal transitions, exact

owner. A forecast alone cannot establish observed fulfillment. External truth cannot

projection bindings and child topology. Using366 at40 allows a later same-phase

closure; current clock ordering is not that policy.

Define separate appraisal and affect outputs, source identities and admissible read
domains; exact rational mathematics; no-output cases; named ablations and failure
ordering. Do not allocate record numbers before those shapes are complete.

Define the eventual character-side task-strategy/action-expectation source before
option construction. The historical fulfillingAction field is not evidence that an
action really achieves a measurement goal. Keep that belief distinct from the
world's execution/effect table, and preserve an interference/failure counterfactual.

## Follow-up substrate finding: content-kind closure

The actual VAL compiler admits only semantic-kind/character through329/330. It
cannot admit a concrete authored commitment simply because generic content/0.2
names commitments as a legitimate authored category. A second kind needs an
accepted deterministic kind specialization and receiving contract; VAL's historical
second-kind positive was explicitly conditional. This is a new frontier dependency,
not reopening the qualification of the existing closed character-only profiles.

Reuse GovernedContentDefinitionId/1038 for authored concrete instance definitions
and the complete authored-origin construction where that future profile admits it.
Do not fake a commitment as a qualified Character, reuse its stakeholder ID, route
a registry definition through an unrelated origin, or invent an unallocated runtime
entity merely to avoid the kind gate. Recurring concrete authored instances can
have distinct StableIds even when their task shape is identical; unrestricted
runtime creation is a separate future extension.

CONTENT_GOVERNANCE explicitly prohibits authored belief/appraisal/affect conclusions.
Accordingly an authored task strategy cannot simply declare a character's efficacy
belief in a content field. Any initial character-side prior belongs to a separately
accepted initial-state/history contract, or must be learned through permitted
experience. That question remains unresolved for option construction. It does not
prevent goal-relative appraisal of the already learned forecast.
