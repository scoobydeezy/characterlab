## Review finding and corrected first target

The accepted `INITIAL_CAUSAL_ROLE_RULE` derives Actor, Target and Participant; it
has no Cause or Incidental mapping. The initial EventRole grammar also supplies no
Incidental binding that could simply be exposed. A missing role cannot repair this.
The [executed SEM component inspection](ATTENTION_SEM_COMPONENT_REVIEW_REV1.json)
constructs three actual perceived bindings and one experience, derives all three
supported claims and rejects foreign-observer evidence. These eight checks establish
component expressibility, not public production.

The first candidate is now **role-priority finite selection** over exactly one distinct
supported role per unit: Actor priority1, Target9/10, Participant3/5. K remains0..2,
with at most3 units. These are candidate calibration values consistent with the
historical role attention table; they are not universal attention mathematics.
Select min(K,n) by decreasing priority then canonical existing unit-key bytes.
Equal-priority and unlimited-selection controls retain separate proposed models.
The positive over-capacity example is Actor/Target/Participant, K1 orK2.

**Residual-pool comparison is DEFERRED**, not passed or silently replaced by
Participant weighting. A future admitted Incidental interpretation and derivation
must precede it. Historical continuous residual allocation, multiplicative salience
and independent/shared/hybrid encoding controls remain retained with their original
meanings. A selector using three fixed supported roles cannot qualify that experiment.

## Actual plural producer proposal

A new finite **observer event profile**, separately admitted from the frozen EMB
factory, owns the source. One physical event at one positive instant, one observer,
up to3 distinct continuants and at most one event-file. World event-role bindings
are authored through a reviewed EventTypeBindingSchema, not per-entity attention
flags. The grammar has bounded Actor/Target/Participant positions. Binding cardinality,
referent domains and exact role visibility are model-governed. A role-swap changes
world participation and its permitted observation, never a raw cognitive score.

Ordered originals may reference the committed event/channel definitions and permitted
physical setup only. They cannot contain SemanticExperience, CausalRoleEvidence,
selected units, arbitrary evidence indexes, attention priorities or output identities.
The exact canonical original-input record layout remains a packaging requirement.
No new source is accepted by the old receiving factory.

Required generated path:

1. At10, observe the bounded event under the channel's permission cut. Produce actual
   observer-safe observations through a separately declared projection; truth bindings
   remain trace-side. Reserve one experience iff at least one character evidence item
   will be emitted. Hidden/denied participants do not enter the character pool.
2. At11, use accepted observer-owned continuant tracking and event-file segmentation
   operations and their existing state owners. Initial empty tracking state, one event
   and up to3 fresh observed tracks suffice for the first finite profile. No public
   original supplies manufactured perceived-file identities or exact truth identity
   continuity. Later continuity/recognition is outside the first fixture.
3. At12, use `projectEventRoleEvidence` and `compilePerceivedBindings` on actually
   observed bindings and supporting observation IDs. Role evidence is exact only when
   the channel establishes it. No category/recognition requirement is inferred.
4. At13, classify independently under an explicitly empty classification domain for
   this finite profile; no invented concepts or skipped required nonempty work.
   At14, freeze the actual assembled experience, settling its reservation once.
5. At15, derive companion claims with the unchanged accepted rule, through the actual
   same-experience evidence domain. Batch up to3 unit requests under a registered
   finite transition and bind all actual completed outputs into the selection input.
   The batch must allocate one existing causal-role occurrence per actual claim;
   selection may not recompute claims from world role names.

For a fully denied/empty observed event there is no experience reservation or empty
fabricated SEM. A distinct **empty observation completion** admission branch carries
only observer/time and the actual source completion identity to phase40; it emits an
explicit EmptyPool selection. For K0 with nonempty perceived evidence, the real SEM
and role records still exist unchanged. Empty perception and capacity exclusion are
therefore distinct, even though both yield no selected reads.

This producer is a proposal for integration of existing operations, not an assertion
that a public factory already composes them. The exact bridge observation projection,
tracking state schema registrations, batch envelopes and input grammar must be in
the symbolic inventory before shape acceptance. Accepted SEM truth cutoffs, conditional
reservation, exact trace ownership and canonical allocation are mandatory.

## Legal schedule and active-control scope

Use existing **phase40, workspace and control allocation**, for selection and the
selected-only diagnostic processing child. Phase15 completion emits selection40;
selection40 emits receipt40 with a strictly later EventSequence. A bounded acyclic
producer→selector→receipt chain is legal under `ordering/0.2-candidate` and
`ordering-phases/2-candidate`. No new phase number or registry version is proposed.
No independent original schedules the receipt. All parent/source coordinates are
bound after authoritative child allocation.

This explicitly narrows the first ATTN experiment to **post-perception active control**.
It does not add a second perception operation to phase20, whose declared purpose is
recognition-input freezing. Existing recognition20/21 remains unchanged; this new
profile need not schedule recognition when no recognition task is admitted. Nothing
in the selection result controls whether an earlier recognition occurred.

It is also not a replacement for task workspace381: its candidates are perceived
units, not prospective commitments or plans. Their future composition needs a named
contract. No appraisal, Decision, learning, consequence lane or later affect feedback
is scheduled here.

## Two carriers, two authorities

Revision1's general selection payload would leak the entire pool if nested in the
consumer input. Separate the full **trace-side SelectionAudit** from the **SelectedEvidenceView**
that actually crosses the consumer boundary. Both are immutable; neither is a new
persistent state family or evidence grammar.

| Proposed surface | Minimum semantic contents and authority |
|---|---|
| EligibleUnitKey | Existing event-file/continuant-file pair; enclosing experience/observer scope. Pure value, no allocated unit ID. |
| EligibleUnit | Key, actual binding references, actual companion references and full distinct-role set. Constructed only from completed same-experience products. |
| AttentionSelectionInput | Either actual completed SEM/companion bundle or empty observation completion. Private admission binding records actual event/model/instant and origin; no caller-built bundle admitted. |
| SelectionAudit | One transition result identity, actual source references, all eligible/excluded units, profile exclusion reasons, exact priorities, K, selected keys and contract version. Full audit is trace-side and not a character lookup capability. Model/run/event details remain in standard trace. |
| SelectedEvidenceView | Source selection occurrence, observer/subject, selected unit keys and only their exact admissible binding/role values or references. No whole SEM, full audit, unselected refs, raw observation envelope or omniscient parent object. No extra occurrence solely for this projection view. |
| SelectedProcessingReceipt | One transition result identity, source selection occurrence and selected unit read receipts. No score-to-strength field, memory payload, reason or learning output. Terminal. |

The diagnostic consumer receives a private one-shot capability containing an immutable
selected-only occurrence index. It has no global archive, body state, task state,
source SEM, generic resolver or callback. Resolve evidence only by the ordinary
closed reference grammar and exact ReadDomain **intersected with this selected index**.
Knowing an unselected occurrence number is insufficient. A role claim may reference
only the selected unit's bindings in this profile; a shared observation reference is
opaque provenance and not permission to open an envelope exposing other units.
Observation-reference dereference is absent from this consumer domain.

Two units sharing a permitted source are not automatically one unit. Duplicate refs
are canonicalized as a set; duplicate identical binding production is rejected by
SEM rather than silently admitted for a duplicate-count test. Multiple distinct
roles on one unit exclude it from this finite profile while preserving its original
claims. Missing role is not Participant or Incidental.

The selector's CharacterId, if required by the exact carrier, must come only from
an accepted observer→subject PRJ/IDN attachment with the matching source observer;
no independent character field in an original is trusted. The consumer inherits
that admitted projection. The implementation must not forge a receiving515 workspace
payload merely to invoke its current PRJ constructor.

## State, failure and replay boundary

Selector and receipt: `WritableStateFamilies = {}`, zero body reads, no RNG. Producer
tracking writes remain solely under the existing SEM state authority and must be
listed separately; this is not a claim that the complete observation run never writes.
Role derivation and attention do not mutate tracking. All source/selection/receipt
occurrences use the accepted runtime allocator and registered output definitions.
Existing namespace domain reuse remains a separate exact inventory review.

At most3 admitted perceived units and3 role claims in the positive first profile;
exact one selection and one receipt per original completion, including empty pool.
Whole-instant rollback includes tracking, reserved experience, all source outputs,
audit, selected capability, trace and allocators. A saved prefix cannot contain a live
consumer capability; replay reconstructs it through the same completed producer and
selection. Canonical save admission must reject edited selected sets, source refs,
capacity, model identity and original visibility.

Exact total work is NOT yet frozen: it depends on whether the registered tracking,
binding and role wrappers are batched or per-item. Count actual events/outputs and
allocator slots from the final symbolic graph; do not infer a number from three
units. No runtime work limit or output schema is allocated by this draft.
