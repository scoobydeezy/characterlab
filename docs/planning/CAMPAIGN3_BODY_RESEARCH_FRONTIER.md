## Authority and research rule

The North Star, Architecture §7.2–7.3 and Research Brief govern this frontier.
Architecture provisionally prefers physiology/regulation through interoception to
embodied pressure. The historical stored Need meter remains a control, not a coequal
default. Keeping ownership experimental does not erase that architectural preference.
Psychological/social motives may have other substrates; this experiment cannot settle
their ownership by analogy to a body variable.

Required phenomenon → public expressibility check → minimum missing seam → reviewed
contract → public fixture → serious competing models → bounded verdict → next gap.
Missing coverage blocks the verdicts it could plausibly invalidate, not the entire
program. Every reduction packet must name absent incoming/outgoing paths and explain
their relevance. Missing affect-to-retrieval can invalidate a broad memory reduction;
it does not automatically block an independent RNG comparison. No general Campaign 4
ranking follows from the present subset of representable families.

## First bounded experiment

Start with one explicitly interpreted embodied quantity, one deterministic exposure
channel and one interoceptive observation channel. Select exact units, kinetics,
interval semantics and time boundaries in a subsequent seam draft; do not borrow
`unit/fixture-pulse` as a physiological unit or introduce conversion by implication.
No hydration, hunger, reward or stress label is accepted merely by choosing a scalar.

The minimum useful fixture must support all these independent contrasts:

1. Equal current levels with different admitted kinetic parameters yield different
   later trajectories. A memoryless level control must have a real chance to fail.
2. Different hidden body states with identical permitted evidence yield identical
   embodied-source outputs when other admitted operands match. Direct truth reading
   fails this control; uncertainty cannot silently collapse to an exact hidden value.
3. Equal body truth with different legitimate interoceptive sensitivity can yield
   different evidence and pressure. No hand-authored pressure intermediate is allowed.
4. A publicly generated embodied source and the existing commitment source address
   the same option. Include both independent motivational grounds and duplicated
   descriptions of shared support; dropping all but one source cannot win by default.

The first fixture need not learn a new persistent Need state, implement affect feedback
or require attention competition. Those remain absent paths to assess when interpreting
its result. If the fixture requires finite-capacity selection, ATTN-001 becomes a real
dependency at that point; it is otherwise a separate contract investigation.

## Source promise before record shapes

The embodied producer must expose its subject through accepted identity admission,
its source family, option/motive meaning and sufficient **observer-safe** causal
ancestry to inspect possible shared support. Reuse admitted evidence references and
occurrences. Hidden common world IDs cannot become overlap keys. A typed reference
must not grant broader evidence-read authority indirectly.

Evidence overlap and independent motivation are separate questions: two motives may
legitimately use the same perceived fact. Do not decide a cross-family normalization,
pairwise maximum or one-die-per-description law here. Preserve MEC-013/014 and
EXP-009/014 controls and review explicit receiving alternatives against both cases.
The existing within-nucleus law remains authoritative in its accepted domain.
