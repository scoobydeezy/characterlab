## Accepted distinctions

LocalReserveId and InteroceptiveSignalId are separate semantic identity families.
The physical runtime address is `(CharacterId, LocalReserveId)`: the local identifier
denotes a declared compartment kind, not a globally unique physical instance. The
three-reserve successor declares its own physical root and source closure. The frozen
singleton EMB profile remains unchanged; exact reserve mathematics may be reused.

The reserve-to-signal bijection belongs only to this synthetic fixture. It creates
no identity equivalence or general physiology rule. Cognition receives safe signal
identities and admitted samples, never physical reserve keys, paths or accessors.

## Experience and evidence boundaries

Replace revision1's reservation condition, “If any permitted view is present,” with:
**reserve a cognitive ExperienceId only when at least one actually admitted/present
observer-side sample exists for that sensing opportunity.** Permission alone is
insufficient. Permitted-but-unavailable, denied and absent views do not create such
a sample. An opportunity with no admitted sample produces neither a cognitive
ExperienceId reservation nor a durable acquisition candidate; diagnostic accounting
may remain trace-side.

One actual sensing opportunity has one ExperienceId shared by its multiple views
and signals. Different opportunities never merge because their SimTime is equal.
Consequence sensing is a separate opportunity with a separate ExperienceId, including
when it occurs in a later phase of the same instant. Shared experience identity must
not manufacture the event-to-outcome relation: that requires the separately governed
fallible attribution assessment.

Support-only SemanticExperience/227 describes available admitted support, not what
survived acquisition. Its support can include observations excluded by selection.
Support identifiers grant no numeric sample lookup authority. Current acquisition
and consequence evidence require separate producer admissions; consequence EVID
remains 227 → 269 → 270. No raw-observation-to-270 shortcut is authorized.

## Ordinary memory and identity review

Interoceptive acquisition is a peer episodic acquisition kind under ordinary memory
lifecycle and authority. It does not fabricate a visual BodyContinuant or create an
independent body-memory sidecar. Its typed representation must participate in retention,
accessibility, retrieval authorization, consolidation, applicable correction and
reconstruction, pruning, persistence and historical inspection. This does not require
one physical array or one schema.

SelectionOccurrenceId/1143 reuse remains **UNRESOLVED** pending an explicit audit of
its accepted semantic role, producers, outputs and retained addressing. A selector
execution identity must not silently become the stable identity of an acquisition.
Neither reuse nor a new MemoryEpisodeId is approved by this receipt. Once the
acquisition occurrence role is settled, selected groups are addressed by
`(AcquisitionOccurrence, InteroceptiveSignalId)`; group membership alone does not
justify a separate runtime occurrence identifier.

Multiple same-instant formations must be collected, deterministically ordered and
reconciled by one memory-family writer under the existing barrier semantics. Two
independent replacements of the same old character leaf are forbidden. Lifecycle B
continues to preserve initial encoding bytes and separate later consolidation.
