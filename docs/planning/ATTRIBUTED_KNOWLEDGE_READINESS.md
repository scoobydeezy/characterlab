## Required distinction
World truth, A's own belief about a proposition, B's actual held belief, and A's
attribution of B's belief must be separately traceable. Calling the latter "knowledge"
must not make a false belief factive or turn lack of evidence into known ignorance.
An observer-safe statement or apparent exposure may support an attribution, but
actual recipient state, hidden receipt flags and privileged execution provenance may not.

## Conservative first question
Can A choose whether/how to explain one fixed proposition based on an explicitly
represented, fallible estimate of what B believes, while A's own belief and adopted
informing goal stay fixed? The role in communication must be exercised behaviorally;
a nested diagnostic with no consumer does not earn the second-order clause.

Use a finite depth and named observer/target/proposition, not unrestricted recursive
theory of mind. Prefer controlled admitted reports or witnessed apparent exposure as
first sources. Name what those observations actually establish, permit misleading
reports/occluded receipt, and keep an unobserved target unknown. If the comparison
needs only evidence of exposure, do not relabel that as evidence of comprehension.

Hold B's actual private state fixed while A receives different attribution evidence;
hold A's safe evidence fixed while B's hidden receipt or actual belief changes. Include
A being wrong, later correction, and two observers attributing differently. Compare
a serious alternative attribution law, a NoPersonModel control and a PrivateStateOracle
violator. Do not equate unknown attribution with a certain opposite belief.
