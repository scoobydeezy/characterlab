# Which retained content receives outcome-significance credit?

2026-09-13. Historical packet; [A subsequently accepted](GENERAL_ATTENTION_SIGNIFICANCE_TARGET_RESOLUTION.md).
Both-direction qualification is accepted and implemented
as a pure component. No significance state or survival rule has been implemented.

## A — only explicitly attributed surviving semantic children (recommended)

Credit the exact extant acquisition/child addresses named as targets by the accepted
attribution-result projection. Co-acquired siblings receive no credit merely through
shared acquisition. Existing surviving-child identity is sufficient; do not allocate
another fragment identity or recover absent content from history.

This resembles use-protection's granularity but has a different source condition:
use protection follows actual consumed operands, while significance credit follows
actual attributed targets plus a qualifying concern assessment. A stationary control
trial may be consumed and earn use protection without being attributed the outcome.
Conversely, a target cannot gain significance merely because it was in that read set.

The current contrastive function outputs Supported/Unavailable only. Its retained
successor records consumed operands, not a qualified causal-target mapping. Therefore
A requires a separate exact target projection from authenticated perceived candidate/
trial connection evidence. A caller-authored address or byte-equal evidence cannot
supply this missing causal role. The source contract must freeze that binding before
an actual credit operation can run.

## B — the whole attributed acquisition

Credit all currently surviving children in the acquisition identified as the outcome's
target, including co-acquired context not individually attributed. This models episode-
wide contextual strengthening. It needs an explicit contextual-spread hypothesis;
atomic acquisition/grouping alone does not establish it. Already lost children still
cannot return. Evidence of use remains independently child-local under either choice.

## Discriminator and adversarial requirements

An old acquisition contains causal-target alpha and incidental sibling beta; a
stationary comparator gamma is in another acquisition. The assessment consumes
alpha and gamma, is Supported, and concern-relative qualification succeeds.
A credits alpha only. B credits alpha and beta. Neither credits gamma solely because
it supplied comparison evidence. Hold use protection constant to isolate this edge.

Repeat with beta already lost, a foreign subject, a missing target, duplicate target
addresses, a nonqualifying concern assessment and false-but-supported attribution.
No truth correction, reconstruction, sibling default or source-observation resolver
is allowed. These are proposed vectors, not executed public proofs.

This decision does not choose storage shape, binary versus graded retained state,
direction-specific effects, saturation, competition with use protection or survival
priority. Those remain the following composed retention-contract questions.

Requested ruling: choose A's explicit attributed-child credit, preserving B as an
episode-context comparator, or authorize B if contextual spread is intended first.
