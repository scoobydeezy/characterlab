places intact-scaffold construction before broad replay and reduction. Expanding a
motive family or closing affect/regulatory loops requires accepted semantics, not
one speculative transition added to make an entry matrix green.

## New behavioral finding

Public baseline versus standing-access-off changes only the governed standing-enabled
Boolean. The four seed pairs preserve identical state and three identity contributions
through time5, and the first three complete decision results match. All six shared
random addresses per pair have identical full draw records. At time6:

| Feedback | Exact option probabilities (contact-one, contact-two) | Mode |
|---|---|---|
|Enabled|1/2,1/2|PlayerFacingRoll|
|Disabled|0,1|Auto|

Seed0 chooses contact-two in both branches; seeds1–3 choose contact-one only with
feedback. Old history entries remain byte-identical after the probe in both branches.
Subsequent new entries differ legitimately because the probe decision changed.

The initial hypothesis that baseline quantization might mask all influence was
falsified. Integer standing modifiers are all zero, but standing also participates
in reason relevance/activation. A weak nonzero base reason survives only with the
earned standing input, changing the option distribution. This is the accepted weak-
motive rescue route, not an unintended private identity die or motivation from zero.
The separate standing-integer component comparisons also expose numeric-modifier
effects, but they are not an unadmitted public combined recipe.

This is a real reference-versus-history-without-feedback experiment. It does not
complete PHEN-BIO's early-only coupling/third-seed probe, sustained contradiction or
broader authorship scope, and it does not show the character is psychologically
sufficient. Raw receipts and the compact checked summary identify those limits.

EMB-001 and the multi-source gap together, while attention/salience has an explicit
parallel planning obligation. This is a proposed research target, not approval of a
