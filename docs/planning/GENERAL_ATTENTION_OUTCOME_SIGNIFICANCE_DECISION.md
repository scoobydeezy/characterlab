## Why a decision remains

GENERAL_ATTENTION_RETENTION_PRIORITY_RESOLUTION requires the old rarely retrieved,
strongly outcome-consolidated memory case. Its last paragraph explicitly accepts no
particular outcome-importance law. GENERAL_ATTENTION_CONSOLIDATION_CREDIT_RESOLUTION
likewise says Supported attribution provides eligibility, not an effect measurement
or write authority. The North Star section11.2 separates importance from accessibility.

The current contrastive component derives a fallible positive-change attribution
from observer-safe intervals. It produces no need, benefit, reward, importance or
expectation-error assessment. The new retained-use consumer earns its binary bit
even on Unavailable, so its bit cannot stand for significant attributed consequences.
Its first fixture changes survival through use alone and does not pass this other case.

## A — concern-relative observed consequences first (recommended direction)

Authorize a separately traceable bounded assessment of an observed consequence
relative to an explicitly represented character concern. Supported attribution
remains the target-eligibility gate; a separate assessment establishes significance.
Begin with a narrowly declared bodily concern, using only admitted observer-safe
evidence and an explicitly governed concern representation. Greater physical change
alone is not greater significance. The same observed change can matter differently
under different concerns, without changing historical encoding or authoritative truth.

This requires a real concern/evaluation seam. Do not relabel TaskConcern, current
pressure, an authored REG reference, or an interoceptive sample as importance merely
because it is available. An OBS↔REG interpretation is not granted implicitly.
Choosing A authorizes investigation of that boundary; exact safe operands, direction,
bounds, missing-evidence behavior, persistence and retention update still need a
shape-complete contract and review before public implementation.

## B — expectation-relative outcome surprise first

Authorize a distinct significance candidate based on discrepancy between a lawful
prior expectation and the admitted experienced consequence, still with the accepted
attribution gate for target credit. A large unexpected neutral consequence could
then earn significance even when it does not advance a current concern. This requires
a properly admitted expectation comparison; current prediction state cannot be
silently repurposed or truth used as an expected/observed substitute.

Both may later coexist. The decision is which causal input to investigate first,
not a reduction of importance to either one or permission to merge their state.

## Discriminating experiment to freeze after the choice

Hold the retained target, age, use count, attribution support and observed consequence
constant. Vary only the admitted concern for A, or only the admitted prior expectation
for B. Show an independent significance/consolidation difference and later retention
difference. Keep the existing use bit equal across these cases, since performing an
attribution task may itself qualify as use. A significance test cannot take credit
for that already-explained edge. Include the old rarely used/newer frequently used
competition, unavailable assessment, false-but-supported attribution and zero-capacity
controls. Do not grant immortality or reconstruct forgotten content.

Still to decide in the chosen contract: which children receive outcome-derived credit,
exact update/composition and its priority relative to binary use protection. No number,
threshold, new importance state, reward law or permanent identity is allocated here.

Recommended ruling: investigate A first, with B retained as a distinct later candidate.
General Attention remains OPEN; the current bounded use-consumer proof remains valid.
