## Minimum proposed action knowledge

Use explicit adopted response instructions: "when this perceived deficit is present,
try this protocol action." An instruction associates a pressure definition with an
action, without an efficacy probability, relief quantity, reward, expected success,
causal belief or value judgment. Its presence in immutable model content does not
make it adopted. A separate initial character binding records adoption.

This follows the bridge already proposed in EMB section6. It is intentionally capable
of being wrong: the protocol has no accepted fuel effect. Later actual world behavior
cannot retroactively license the instruction or create one from registry visibility.
Quantitative NeedExpectation remains a comparison/future seam, not an implicit field.

## Ground and overlap boundary

Keep one395 option identity. Task ground is its actual371 key; body ground is the
projected CharacterId plus the admitted pressure definition. Body instructions are
descriptions/origins of that ground, not additional appetites. Two adopted instructions
for one pressure/action preserve both origins but must not yield two independent dice.
Task and body grounds remain distinct even when they support the same action.

The candidate receiving law is the existing aggregate coverage law *within* an exact
ground/role/sign. It does not discount an independent commitment merely because its
action equals a body's action. Compare it with global collapse, source deletion and
no-overlap alternatives. This earns no universal cross-family normalization verdict.
