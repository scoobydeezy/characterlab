# Ordinary memory retention: first cross-kind resource decision

2026-09-12. **DECISION REQUIRED; no retention policy accepted here.** This packet
follows [carrier inspection](ATTENTION_ACQUISITION_CARRIER_DRAFT_REV1.md). The accepted
separate gates, shared acquisition identity and ordinary-memory authority stay closed.

## The unresolved choice

Should new event/continuant and interoceptive acquisitions compete for a common
durable retention capacity in the first profile, or have explicit per-kind retention
budgets managed by the same ordinary-memory authority?

Separate acquisition gates answer what gets through selection. They do not answer
what later loses durable individuality when storage is bounded. The source review
requires ordinary retention/access/consolidation/pruning services but explicitly left
retention uncommitted. Shared AcquisitionOccurrenceId likewise defines addressing,
not a resource pool or a common importance scale.

The [visual retention draft](ATTENTION_MEMORY_RETENTION_REVIEW_REV1.md) has at most
four insert-only encodings in a fixed experiment. It supplies no eviction or fragmentation
law and no body-versus-visual comparison. Merely increasing that list's bound or
rejecting every full-memory input would demonstrate a finite fixture, not qualify
ordinary retention/pruning. Its old episode/presentation/graph invariants cannot be
silently applied to bodies, whose content has no continuant graph nodes.

North Star11 and Architecture8 distinguish retention, accessibility and recognition,
require progressive memory change and preserve defining individuality. They do not
mandate either a unified capacity or protected modality quotas. The Research Brief
explicitly rejects assuming memory is just immutable records plus retrieval scores.
MEC-010's recency/frequency/accessibility control remains required but cannot be used
as an eviction importance law without its own declared hypothesis.

## Concrete alternatives

**A — shared retention competition now.** One explicit retention resource admits
both acquisition kinds. The successor must declare its common accounting unit and
cross-kind survival/fragmentation policy. Body acquisition can displace visual memory
even with both gates held fixed. This is a legitimate new causal path, but it requires
an explicit comparison law; raw acquisition count, payload byte size and psychological
importance are not interchangeable. Different payload schemas do not block a common
pool, and the pool does not require a common evidence-strength scalar.

**B — bounded per-kind budgets first (recommended).** One ordinary-memory lifecycle
authority manages explicitly partitioned event/continuant and interoceptive retention
budgets. There is no cross-kind eviction in this first model. Same-kind pressure,
retention versus accessibility, later consolidation and applicable pruning must still
be witnessed; this is not permission for two hidden memory universes or retain-all
archives. Both kinds use acquisition addressing and the same conceptual authorized
read/history/persistence services, with typed policies where semantics differ.

B is a bounded control choice, not a claim that human memory has independent modality
stores. Keep A as a named later comparison, just as shared acquisition/control was
preserved. Fixed partitions need no invented cross-kind importance exchange rate;
their cost is that this first model cannot demonstrate body-driven eviction of a
visual acquisition. Common selection or retrieval resources remain separate questions.

## Distinguishing witness

An illustrative two-entry budget shows why a layout choice would silently choose
behavior. Start with one older visual acquisition V and one body acquisition B1,
then admit B2 through unchanged gates. With a *hypothetical* shared oldest-first
policy, V can disappear. With one slot per kind and the same within-kind oldest-first
policy, B1 disappears and V survives. Total storage count is two in both cases.
This is a design counterexample, not an accepted FIFO law, public test result or
proposed permanent parameter. Later cueing V distinguishes the models without
changing source evidence or selection.

Any actual experiment must separately declare importance/fragmentation and show that
retention is not simply copied from accessibility. Admission limits must be separated
from modeled forgetting: rejecting an out-of-profile run is not a cognitive loss.

## Invariants and work after the decision

Either choice preserves selected-only content, acquisition identities, source
provenance, sole-authority reconciliation, immutable historical initial encoding and
append-only later interpretation history. Pruning may remove character-accessible
content but cannot make historical trace a sample resolver or allow acquisition-ID
reuse. The exact bounded replay/anti-duplicate mechanism must survive permitted
pruning without an unbounded private tombstone archive.

After the ruling: specify the finite retention/fragmentation control, its importance
basis, empty/full behaviors and comparisons; complete child carriers and ordinary
read services; recompute output/state bounds and persistence obligations. This packet
does not ask to reopen identity B, separate acquisition gates, attribution eligibility
or the three-reserve source. No permanent allocation or public implementation occurs
before those concrete contracts are ready.
